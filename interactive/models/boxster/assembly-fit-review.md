# Car-to-assembly fit review

Reviewed sequentially on 11 November 2026. One writer changed the shared car and chapter assemblies. Scope: the fifteen car-to-component 3D tours, chapters 04–18. Other interactive figures are functional schematics without an installed car overlay.

The error in the roof example came from independently authored surfaces: the car's upper windscreen centre is at `[0, 1.175, .12]`, whereas the chapter's old upper rail was approximately `[0, 1.22, .52]`. These are **model coordinates in metres**, not measured Porsche fitting dimensions. Blending both meshes made the error visible during the first part of the tour.

The car's photo-guided exterior was retained. Visible component shapes now use shared factories or the car's actual exported meshes. Detailed assemblies are installed from the first frame; duplicate context geometry is removed once. Only the surrounding car fades before the explosion, so a zoom never substitutes a different shape. The 2010 standard manual and optional PDK selectors remain distinct variants.

## Evidence and limits

Owning chapter research, photo credits and factory PET reference sets remain the source of component identities and variants. The car's photo-guided outer envelope is the installation reference for this review. PET exploded drawings do not establish exact coordinates, hidden routing, clearances or motion calibration; those remain illustrative. No factory dimensional accuracy or service-fitting instructions are claimed.

Verification includes the shipped GLB (not only authoring groups), identical visible surface vertices, unchanged world transforms when transferring car meshes, bonnet clearance at the front mount, all fifteen tours at 0/8/30/65/100, and responsive reader checks. Static posters use the same installation path as the interactive view. The single Pages build and existing whole-book print fallbacks remain the delivery format.
