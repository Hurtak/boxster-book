# Reference appendix: evidence and limits

Added 10 November 2026. This appendix implements the coverage review: dated annual/derivative matrix, visual wheel/seat/palette atlas, audio reference, option index and glossary. Baseline is UK ordinary 2010 2.9; original 18-chapter sequence is retained.

The existing primary-source ledgers in chapters 2, 3, 11–18 were read before consolidation. The original ordinary UK 01/10 PDF was re-extracted and relevant spreads were rendered: PDF 34 (instruments), 35 (seats), 48 (palette), 50 (wheels), 52–54 (trim). Its 01/10 GB/UK identity remains unchanged. Photos/samples were matched visually to their source captions. New evidence: p.101's prospective September-2010 colours and through-July colours, preserving the earliest-date qualifier and the source’s “Artic” spelling in the evidence note. This is not an observed production change.

The UK launch 08/08 and ordinary 01/10 sources do not supply a complete UK 2011/2012 order-guide sequence. A fresh search found the auto-brochures archive’s 2011 file to be explicitly US; it is not promoted into UK evidence. Another [archive entry labelled “2011 – Boxster (UK)”](https://manuals.plus/m/d62063d0453dfdef9194200b3dd1bcb710e883a1bae1b5aa5ef4d49a32fb9318) contains the later 981: 2.7-litre engine, electric parking brake and dynamic gearbox mounts. Its colophon says edition 11/2011, WSLB1301000125 GB/UK, copyright 2012. Its publication date does not make it a 2011 987.2 order guide, so it is excluded from this matrix.

U entries remain explicit and separate from S/O/N. US mechanical corroboration cannot establish UK annual equipment or every part revision. No prices, unverified effective dates, numerical option discounts or parts interchanges are invented. Original model-year/announcement boundaries remain in chapter 2.

The glossary and code index point to existing owning chapters. They introduce no new mechanical specifications. New trim/code rows are transcribed from the original UK 01/10 pp. 106–118; the special-model-only, North American XCZ and later-PET XLS scopes remain visible. The S’s PCCB availability is not assigned to the 2.9.

Image extraction and exact object mappings are recorded in images/credits.md. Samples are catalogue reproductions, not calibrated colours, paint formulations or a verified owner-car specification. Standard seat operation is illustrated by the existing original schematic; photographs show the named optional families. The original wheel/seat photographs have modest native resolution; they were not upscaled to invent detail.
