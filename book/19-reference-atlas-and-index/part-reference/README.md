## Interpretation and rights

- The dated UK brochure/price list establishes sales names, eligibility, packages and the named date's price. PET provides service identifiers and assembly relationships. Standard parts also have PR identifiers: 394 is the base wheel and 480 the manual gearbox. Left/right seat codes identify the car's sides; 098/099 distinguishes LHD/RHD.
- The PET key is generic: it includes earlier engines, Tiptronic, unrelated wheel sizes, sliding-roof and other-model equipment. Its page 9 text for PR 220 says 40%; model-specific Porsche training p. 86 and the UK Black Edition list instead give **22% drive / 27% overrun**, which remain authoritative. No generic-key entry overrides the model-specific account.
- PET application/mounting dates and model-year replacement splits do not establish announcement, first delivery, paint eligibility, original 2010 prices, exact installed geometry, material, movement, control calibration or interchangeability. Its representative wheel/seat drawing cannot establish every named design or material. A removable centre cap does not establish the wheel or car's original specification.
- Catalogue colour samples remain uncalibrated reproductions. Original eligibility and combinations come from dated market documents; no paint formula or colour-order code is inferred from these parts pages.
- All factory drawings, photographs and swatches remain **Porsche copyright**. These excerpts are reference reproductions; the project's MIT licence does not relicense them. The imported seat-support SVG is separate original project artwork.
