# Reference atlas and index

Use this appendix alongside the component chapters. The **2010 European/UK Boxster 2.9** remains the first reference: standard equipment and factory options are separated, while other years and derivatives are explicitly identified. The tables collect evidence rather than assigning options to a particular owner’s car.

## Annual equipment matrix

**S** means standard, **O** optional, **N** unavailable and **U** unverified for the stated market/date. A catalogue’s presence in a column establishes its published specification; it does not date the first delivery. **US corroboration** supports the named broad mechanical feature only. It cannot turn a U into a confirmed UK equipment entry.

| System | 2010 UK 2.9 reference · 01/10 catalogue | 2009 introduction evidence | 2011 ordinary UK 2.9 | 2012 ordinary UK 2.9 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Engine | S · 2,893 cm³, port injection, 188 kW / 255 PS | Same headline specification in launch catalogue | U · annual UK component revisions | U · UK details; US sheet corroborates capacity/output/injection |
| Gearbox | S · six-speed manual; O · seven-speed PDK | Six-speed/PDK replace base five-speed/Tiptronic choices | U · annual UK ordering changes | U · UK details; US sheet corroborates transmission choices |

## Wheel atlas

Start with the 2.9’s Boxster III wheel. The remaining nine designs are January 2010 UK catalogue options for the ordinary 2.9; the S’s standard 18-inch wheel is an option here. Spoke design identifies an appearance, while the casting’s width/offset and approved tyre combination establish fitment. The [wheel chapter](../12-steering-wheels-and-tyres/index.mdx#reading-a-wheel-and-tyre-size) explains those dimensions.

The parts catalogue identifies the standard design as **PR 394**, with distinct front and rear wheels. Its centre cap is a separate part: a coloured crest alone cannot establish the wheel design or the car’s original equipment. The factory parts drawing uses a representative wheel, so the labelled period photographs below remain the design reference.

| Seat | 2010 UK code · PET PR left / right | Useful distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Standard; PR 369 / 370 | Manual fore/aft and height; electric backrest; Alcantara centres |
| Sports | P77 · optional; PR 375 / 376 | Same basic adjustment pattern; firmer upholstery and larger bolsters |

The PR pairs identify seat families in the service catalogue; the P-codes identify the dated sales packages. Memory, ventilation and upholstery require their own records. Left and right always mean the car’s sides: the right seat is the driver’s seat in the UK RHD reference, and its runners differ from the corresponding LHD application.
