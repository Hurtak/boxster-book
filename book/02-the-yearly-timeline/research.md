# Chapter 02 research: the yearly timeline

Checked 10 November 2026. Scope is the European/UK 987.2 chronology, with the model-year 2010 2.9 as the reference car. `model-year-research.md` supplied leads; the following contemporary primary sources were independently opened. US equipment is not used as UK equipment evidence. No full sequence of ordinary-model UK annual price lists was recovered.

## Remaining limits

- No complete annual UK ordinary-model price-list sequence, colour list, running software history or factory component change-point chronology. Absence of a recovered change is not proof that every detail remained unchanged.
- No exact last-987 build day or first UK 981 customer-delivery day. Dutch April date is labelled by market.
- No individual owner's gearbox, build date, model year or option list assumed.
- The old international Porsche launch URL redirects to the current newsroom; dated Porsche Netherlands and the contemporary SE PDF are the surviving primary chronology evidence used here.
