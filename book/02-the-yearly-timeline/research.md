- The Dutch Spyder, Black Edition and 981 releases give prospective February 2010, March 2011 and 14 April 2012 dates. Prose and static/interactive labels now say planned/announced introduction with market context, rather than treating the releases as proof of completed customer arrival. The 2009 ordinary-range arrival remains supported by the later Porsche SE release, p. 2, which reports availability since February 2009.
