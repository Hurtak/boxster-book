## `2009-launch-boxster-s.jpg`

- Subject: red facelift **Boxster S**, side profile. It is a comparison car, not the book's reference 2.9.
- Origin: Porsche, *The new Boxster*, UK edition **08/08**, WVK 314 725 09 GB/UK, © Porsche AG 2008.
- Location: PDF page 6 of 63, unnumbered photographic spread between printed pp. 10 and 12.
- Exact source: <https://porschecarshistory.com/wp-content/old/brochures5/26/2009%20987%20Boxster%20UK.pdf#page=6>.
- Downloaded and verified 10 November 2026. Extracted with `pdfimages -f 6 -l 6 -j`; original JPEG retained without crop, text overlay, colour alteration or recompression. Brochure contains the photograph across its whole spread; the exported object contains no brochure text.
- Limits: visible wheels, brakes and trim do not establish 2.9 standard equipment or another car's options.

- Origin: Porsche, *The Boxster Spyder*, UK edition **01/10**, WSLB1101000225 GB/UK, © Porsche AG 2010.
- Location: printed p. 15, right-hand photograph on PDF page 6 of 24.
- Exact source: <https://porschecarshistory.com/wp-content/old/brochures2/93/2010%20987%20Boxster%20Spyder.pdf#page=6>.
