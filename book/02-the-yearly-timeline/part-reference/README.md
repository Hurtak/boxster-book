## Chronology and interpretation

The catalogue was compiled in **2018**, after the cars it covers. Its model overview places Spyder and Black Edition sales types in an `08/10–05/12` “mounting time” interval. That interval is not substituted for Porsche's contemporary November 2009 Spyder or February 2011 Black Edition announcements. Likewise, the Spyder hood list's `MJ2010 >>` entry does not settle the numerical European model year. These are differing catalogue fields; no attempt is made here to turn them into a factory production ledger.

Announcement dates and planned market introductions remain grounded in [the dated Porsche sources in the chapter research](../research.md). Parts drawings identify assemblies and their neighbours. They do not establish original UK option availability, supersession timing, forces, materials, control behaviour, performance or workshop removal order.
