## Option groups and conditions

**Printed pp. 42–47**: PDK manual/automatic modes, gears 1–6 sports ratios, top speed reached in sixth, seventh economical; basic PDK wheel uses shift switches; optional paddle wheel is distinct. Detailed transmission construction belongs elsewhere.

**Printed pp. 56–57 and 107**: Sport Chrono **639**; Sport Chrono Plus **640**, explicitly **only with PCM**. PDK supplies the context for Launch Control and the SPORT PLUS shift strategy. Chrono alone does not establish PDK, PCM or PASM. No claim that Chrono increases rated engine output.

**Printed p. 107 / PDF page 49**: PDK **250** and PASM **475** offered on both ordinary models; PASM lowers ride height 10 mm. Differential **220** has **18- or 19-inch wheel** condition. The **January 2010** text at both pp. **54** and **107** does not repeat the launch **08/08** brochure's manual-only qualifier or future availability date. The chapter flags that difference precisely and does not categorically promise eligibility for every build date. PCCB **450** shows a dash for 2.9 and an availability circle for S; therefore not a catalogue 2.9 option. Sports exhaust **XLF** is separately offered.

**Printed pp. 106–118**, selected codes independently checked in text and rendered table: bi-xenon/cornering **P75**; dimming mirrors/rain **P12**; ParkAssist **635**; climate **573**; cruise **454**; wind deflector **551**; hardtop **550**; comfort seats **P15**; sports **P77**; adaptive sports **P01**; buckets **P03**; heating **342**; PCM/navigation **P23**; Sound Package Plus **490**; BOSE **680**; universal interface **870**. The latter code appears for different head-unit interface arrangements; chapter does not assert all interfaces provide USB on CDR-30.

**Printed pp. 74–76**: baseline seats manual fore/aft/height, electric backrest; standard air conditioning, optional automatic climate control; CDR-30 standard at p. **82**. Ventilation prose at p. **75** adds conditions beyond p.111's short row: standard/comfort seats, heated seats, automatic climate and part/full leather. Storage box is absent with BOSE, p. **76**. Reader is taught to read the explanatory text as well as the code row.

## Record-reading limits

Owner manual printed p. **274** lists VIN, type/type description, engine/transmission codes, paint/interior and optional equipment on the Maintenance-booklet label. Chapter deliberately avoids claiming this label is a complete immutable list of every production identifier or that a missing label proves misconduct. No invented VIN, owner build sheet, option selection, paint code or model-year decoding is supplied.

The practical three-column record method (factory evidence / present equipment / later invoices) is editorial guidance, not a Porsche-issued workflow. Generic decoder results are treated as leads requiring the correct market/model/date source. No promise of current availability or price for a Porsche record service; no exhaustive unsupported prices.
