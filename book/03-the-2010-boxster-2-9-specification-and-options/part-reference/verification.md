## Changes, checks and unresolved limits

Changes are deliberately small: added the port-injection table row and the model-specific PET identity paragraph, expanded the existing record footnote, archived selected original pages and documented full coverage. Existing performance/weight/option diagrams already agree with their owning primary documents, so no geometry, animation, SVG or poster was altered. No additional explorer is needed for this context chapter.

All **18 footnote-reference occurrences in their original order**, **14 definitions**, **8 chapter/section headings** and **2 diagram IDs** are preserved. Both JS modules pass `node --check`; all three SVGs parse as XML; local image/import targets and reference-archive links exist. No shared files, global builds, browser sessions or git staging were used. Parent integration owns reader rendering at phone/tablet/desktop sizes. No poster regeneration is required.

Unresolved: a complete dated ordinary-model UK price-list sequence, exact differential/PDK ordering transition, and an individual car's authenticated factory record. The later replacement-parts snapshot cannot fill those gaps. Exact installation coordinates, damping calibration, control timing, dynamic forces and interchangeability of complete assemblies are not established by these drawings.
