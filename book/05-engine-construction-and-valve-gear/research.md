Checked 10 November 2026. Baseline: European/UK model-year 2010 Boxster 987.2 2.9. Porsche-authored publications are the technical authorities; a seller's own photographs document only the photographed donor. No workshop procedure or parts-interchange claim is inferred from the diagrams.

Their Wright engine is a mechanism teaching source, not Porsche fitment evidence.

Chapter's torque/rpm conversion and numerical examples are calculations, not a measured output curve.

## Car-to-engine 3D explorer

`diagrams/engine-explorer/` locates the reference 2.9 behind the seats, ahead of the rear axle, then separates eight assemblies: sectioned crankcase, crankshaft, six pistons/rods, two heads, four camshafts, intake and exhaust manifolds. The model retains six separate crankpins, staggered banks, horizontal bores, two cams per head and four valves per cylinder. Curved intake runners approach the heads from above; exhaust branches leave below. Two timing chains are described as a connection but deliberately omitted geometrically so this cutaway does not invent their physical route.

Evidence checked 10 November 2026: Porsche's [flat-engine account](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en_US/technology/porsche-flat-engine-sports-car-tradition-design-principle-internal-combustion-engine-lightweight-power-dynamic-15767.html) freshly confirms the opposed, offset banks. Freshly indexed Porsche [2009 Technik Introduction](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3550487/Porsche-Boxster-2009.html), printed Engine pp. 9–18 / viewer pp. 15–24, confirms crank/rod/head/cam/chain architecture; Fuel and ignition p. 59 / viewer p. 65 confirms the 2.9 bank-specific inlet runners. The direct viewer page requests failed, so the check uses indexed primary-document text, not a claimed new visual inspection of those pages. The existing local related-Cayman 2.9 engine photograph was inspected for the upper intake and lower crankcase context; it does not establish exact Boxster dimensions.

The cylinder rings depict sections through an integrated casting, not separate air-cooled barrels or removable iron liners. Crank angles, rod positions, head/valve shapes and runner/manifold routes are illustrative. Cooling jackets, solid casting walls, timing chains, covers, wiring and accessories are omitted; open frames are cutaways, not the exterior of the castings. Separation is a display aid, not piston movement, timing-setting information or a workshop disassembly sequence.
