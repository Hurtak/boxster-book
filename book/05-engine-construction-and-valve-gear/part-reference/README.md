## Application rules and limits

- **A120** is PET's 2.9 application label; **A121** is the 3.4 application. The 2009 training publication uses **MA120/MA121** and separately names **MA120C/MA121C** for Cayman. Preserve those source-specific designations. The Boxster catalogue is not a complete Cayman application check.
- Catalogue PR **480** denotes the six-speed manual (PDF p. 11); **250** denotes PDK (p. 9); **778** denotes Spyder (p. 13); **XLF** denotes sports exhaust (p. 16). These are application conditions in a replacement-parts catalogue, not evidence that an option was originally orderable in every 2010 market.
- Distinct A120 and A121 crankcase, piston, head and camshaft lines support the chapter's model distinction. A common later replacement number elsewhere does not prove an identical original part, original calibration or complete-assembly interchangeability. Discontinued and alternative lines must be read with their conditions.
- The head-cover and seal lists contain paired engine-number conditions; this reference preserves them rather than inventing a universal model-year change. A complete 2009–2012 UK running-change record remains unresolved.
- PET confirms component identity, quantity and exploded assembly relationships. Its separation lines are not installation coordinates, a belt/chain routing instruction, a timing-setting procedure or an order of disassembly. Materials, bearing construction, lift, phase range, valve events and controller behaviour are established by the Porsche technical sources in [chapter research](../research.md), not deduced from the parts silhouettes.
- Main-bearing shell quantities and colour classes are inventory selections. They are not a direction to choose clearances; eight crankshaft bearing positions are established by the training publication.
- Two cam-drive chains and two oil-fed cam-chain tensioners are distinct from the additional oil-pump chain and its guide/tensioning blade. No intermediate shaft is introduced by the oil-pump drive.
- Full intake, fuel, ventilation, lubrication, cooling, exhaust and mount explanations remain with their owning chapters. Their adjacent assemblies are retained here to verify this chapter's connections and visible engine outline, without reproducing their complete systems.
