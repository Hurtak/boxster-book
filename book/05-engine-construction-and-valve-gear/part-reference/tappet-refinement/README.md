# Inlet tappet shape and mechanism references

Checked 14 November 2026. Reference car: 2010 European/UK Boxster 2.9, MA120 / PET A120. The revised figure compares the real exterior with explanatory internal sections; it does not claim measured internal dimensions.

## Actual part photographs

Wolf Auto Parts, [Intake Cam Follower (INLET) 9A110507003, PS60678](https://wolfautoparts.com/parts/intake-cam-follower-inlet-9a110507003-ps60678/). The seller's own product photographs were retrieved from its page and visually inspected. The page identifies the listed part number; no donor-car record was established. PET 987209/P33, 19 July 2018, **103-010**, PDF **pp. 50–51**, lists item **9**, hydraulic tappet, intake, **9A1 105 070 03**, quantity **12**, A120/A121. This supports catalogue identity without transferring current replacement availability or complete interchangeability claims.

The photographs show a cylindrical bucket, a small circular central contact face within the broad outer face, an open underside, concentric internals and side holes/locating details. The original diagram's tall rectangular blocks and disconnected two-lobe layout were discarded. The new exterior envelope is 280 drawing units wide and about 200 high (0.71 height/diameter), with a 126-unit central contact pad (0.45 of diameter). These are photo-guided illustration ratios, not calibrated physical dimensions. Perspective and the used component's orientation preclude a dimensional tolerance claim. Side-hole positions are not invented as exact oil-gallery geometry.

- `uk-01-10-p16-variocam.jpg`: unchanged embedded image, object **56 0**, **399 × 367**, Porsche AG, *The Boxster*, UK **01/10**, WSLB1101000125, **printed p. 36 / PDF p. 16**. [Original PDF](https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf#page=16). Visually inspected against the complete spread. Establishes the three neighbouring inlet cam tracks, a concentric cutaway tappet and a separate external valve spring. Does not establish internal pin dimensions.
- `p001-p48-tappet-cutaway.png`: full-page render of Porsche AfterSales Training, *General Servicing & Repair – Sports Cars*, **P001**, ©2007, **printed 1.24 / PDF p. 48**. [Primary publication, independent host](https://souzaweb.com/caymanregister/downloads/54580-General_Porsche_Repair_%28P001_-_Book_5-07%29.pdf#page=48). The page's example is an earlier **996 Carrera** system, not a MA120 component drawing. Its explicit cutaway and text corroborate concentric members, lateral pin, internal spring and hydraulic clearance compensation. Neither its older 11 mm peak nor its exact interior geometry is transferred to the 2.9. Full page retained without cropping or replacement callouts, rendered at 1600 px longest side.
- Porsche Engineering Magazine **02/2005**, printed/PDF **p. 16**, re-read: concentric lifters, locking pin, inner/small and outer/large cam action, and hydraulic clearance compensation. [Original publication](https://porschecarshistory.com/wp-content/old/Porsche_engineering/PE-02.2005.pdf#page=16). This is generic principle evidence; neither the older controller nor an example lift specification is adopted.
- Baseline peaks **3.6 / 10.0 mm** remain from Porsche *2009 Technik Introduction*, Engine **p. 16 / viewer p. 22**, already documented in the chapter research and source note. No new opening-angle curve, switching rpm or lift calibration is introduced.

## Diagram decisions and limits

`variocam-tappet-uncoupled.svg` and `variocam-tappet-coupled.svg` are original vector teaching drawings. Identical exterior dimensions and shaft positions are used in both states. Two outer cam tracks flank the small centre track on a shared shaft; all are shown near a common base-circle position. One visible pin stays within the inner member in the uncoupled view and bridges the inner/outer boundary in the coupled view. Hydraulic clearance compensation and the return spring are drawn inside the bucket. A break in the continuing valve stem avoids presenting a shortened full valve as a scale drawing; the valve's own spring is outside this cutaway.

The two sections depict coupling, not simultaneous maximum lifts. Profile silhouettes, bore clearances, internal spring geometry, pin dimensions and the oil-control arrow remain schematic. No pin-return spring, internal ball/check valve, oil-drilling map, cam timing, workshop setting or measured wear limit is invented. The photographic comparison retains source pixels; numbered feature keys and all explanatory text are separate manuscript/layout elements.
