## Reading these sheets

The reference car remains the **2010 European/UK 2.9**, with six-speed manual standard and seven-speed PDK optional. These roof/glass pages do not create a transmission-specific roof variant. Ordinary Boxster/S architecture is kept separate from **Spyder PR:778** and **hardtop PR:550**. PR:098/099 are LHD/RHD, not gearbox options.

The service snapshot establishes component identities, quantities, side allocation and the pictured assembly relationships. It does not prove the part suffix originally installed on a particular 2010 car, current supply, entire assembly interchangeability, annual option/colour availability or introduction dates. In particular the filter production date comes from Porsche **TI 29/09 ENU 5393**, not from the later PET filter row; its wording and period UK option evidence remain in [research.md](../research.md).

Exploded views are neither CAD nor workshop dismantling sequences. They do not establish exact pivots, hidden hose/shaft routing, cloth folds, tolerances, seal compression, control timing, motor calibration, pan capacity or physical forces. The chapter's two sequence/function diagrams and nine-group 3D cutaway retain those limits visibly. The separate compartment hoses and side-pan pipes are not joined into a guessed complete drain circuit.
