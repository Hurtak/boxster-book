## Resulting geometry and boundaries

- One fixed **624-unit** side envelope runs from x=26 to x=650. Axles are x=183.15 and x=530.22: wheelbase/length **0.55620**, matching **2415/4342 = 0.55620**. The body contour follows the existing photo-guided family illustration at **624/544** scale, replacing the low pointed ends and short bonnet of the earlier roof drawing. The overhang split remains a photographic approximation, not an independently verified front/rear measurement. Closed roof height remains approximately 186 units above the ground, following the nominal 1292/4342 height/length ratio.
- Nominal tyre outside diameters are derived from the specified fitment: front `431.8 + 2 × 205 × 0.55 = 657.3 mm`, rear `431.8 + 2 × 235 × 0.50 = 666.8 mm`. At 624/4342 scale, the drawn radii are **47.23 / 47.91 units**. These are undeformed nominal envelopes, not loaded tyre measurements or a suspension-setting specification.
- The fixed screen meets the higher body shoulder; the seat/head restraint now reclines rearwards, with its upper portion behind the seat base. The steering wheel has an inclined rim, hub and short column, while the dashboard is a thin section profile. The unrelated exterior-mirror silhouette and filled dashboard block have been omitted to avoid unexplained cabin shapes. Fabric, lining, articulated supports and pivot symbols remain separate. The rigid pane remains **62 × 8 drawing units in every snapshot**; only its whole-body position/orientation changes. The thin pane is nearly edge-on in this side section, not a representational measurement of its width across the car.
- The cover rises locally behind the bar while the rear boot lid stays fixed. The folded package is below the closed cover in the cutaway well behind the seats. That centreline cutaway omits near-side bodywork and overlaps the projected near-side wheel region; it is not a lateral installation/clearance drawing.
- Five labelled snapshots and the existing `data-roof-*` state contracts remain unchanged. Four poster snapshots are generated from the same `roofArtwork` and `roofCallouts` sources. Poster leaders share the artwork's transform, keeping their endpoints on the revised parts. Window lowering remains an illustrative clearance state, without inventing an exact lowering trigger or measured drop.

**Limits:** photos and exploded PET views do not provide CAD surfaces, measured pivot coordinates, crank angles, bow/seam count, pane aperture dimensions, exact cloth folds, seal cross-sections/compression, well volume, hidden shaft routes or calibrated roof/cover/window timing. Small joints, seal lines and fabric/lining layers identify supported functions; their profiles and coordinates are illustrative. The apparent intermediate poses are explanatory snapshots with overlapping real movements, not a workshop sequence or kinematic reconstruction. Use the correct handbook for operation.
