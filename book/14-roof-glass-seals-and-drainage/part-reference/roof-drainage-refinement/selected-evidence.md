# Selected observations

- **812-000, p. 418:** pan 12 is a shallow, irregular open moulding; the high wing and scalloped edge distinguish it from a generic tank. Filter 24 is a broad mesh cap, not a small cylindrical plug. Pipe 13 bends below the outlet. The drawing is exploded and does not establish installed dimensions.
- **TI 29/09, Figure 1:** the first panel identifies the bare drain opening beside the mechanism; the second places a mesh filter over that opening. The drawing does not show the complete downstream pipe route.
- **811-012, pp. 410–411:** plate 25 is a separate long flanged piece alongside the roof drive/guide assembly. Its factory name is “Water drain plate”.
- **811-013, pp. 412–413:** item 9 is a compartment seal, with its own two drainage hoses at item 10. The exact table wording is “seal for conv. top stowage box”. This is not another pan or plate 25.

These observations were checked against the actual pictures as well as the tables. See [provenance.md](provenance.md) for source URLs, the retained original figure and photograph, applicability, rights and unresolved geometry.
