# Roof, glass, seals and drainage: evidence and limits

Research checked 10 November 2026. Baseline: MY2010 Boxster 987.2 2.9, Europe/UK. Porsche-authored documents are primary evidence even where a collector or workshop archive hosts the copy. Web search and image search were run freshly; brochure PDFs were downloaded, text-extracted and relevant pages rendered. All five selected local JPEGs were inspected. This note records findings, not workshop instructions.

## Verified reference-car claims

- **Ordinary roof:** fabric roof with inner lining, heated glass rear window, quoted opening time 12 seconds and operation to 50 km/h. Porsche, *The Boxster*, UK edition 01/10, printed pp. 80–81, [scan p. 38](https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf#page=38). Front latch is manual; powered travel does not lock it for the driver. Porsche's MY2010 North American owner manual, WKD 987 021 10, 5/09, printed pp. 182–185 ([PDF](https://billswebspace.com/Owners_Manual_Boxster_PCNA.pdf#page=184)). US document used for operation, not UK standard-equipment claims.
- **Operating boundaries:** owner manual printed p. 182 lists ignition on, maximum 50 km/h, no operation below 0°C, no obstructing objects, adequate clearance and even support. Roof messages are on printed p. 168. Chapter gives the ceiling but directs normal operation to the handbook, with no bypasses, emergency disconnection sequence or calibration instructions.
- **Drive architecture:** Porsche PET 987209, model life 2009–2012, snapshot 19 July 2018, illustration 811-012 ([scan pp. 410–411](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf#page=410)): ONE electric motor 986 624 117 01; TWO pliable shafts 987 561 107 00; left/right gearboxes 987 561 179 03 / 180 03; rods, guide arms, hinge levers and bushes. This corrects misleading generic descriptions of two roof motors. Illustration 811-005 gives frame/bracing components. A shaft transmits rotation; it should not be described as an ordinary Bowden pull cable.
- **Switch evidence:** Porsche WM 616419 identifies a microswitch at the ordinary roof front lock; [2010 987 S archive](https://charm.li/Porsche/2010/Boxster%20S%20%28987%29%20F6-3.4L/Repair%20and%20Diagnosis/Sensors%20and%20Switches/Sensors%20and%20Switches%20-%20Body%20and%20Frame/Hard%20Top%20Position%20Switch%20%2F%20Sensor/Service%20and%20Repair/61641900%20Removing%20and%20installing%20microswitch%20for%20roof/). Its location is used narrowly. No count/location map of all roof limit switches or earlier 986 relay logic is claimed.
- **Glass:** owner manual printed p. 18 states door-window lowering by a few millimetres on opening and raising on closing. Printed pp. 27–28 refer to automatic lowering, roof operation and stored window end position. PET illustrations 804-020 and 804-030 ([scan pp. 286, 288](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf#page=288)) identify window-regulator microswitches at door handle and separate electric regulator motors/cable components. Chapter does not invent a short-drop distance or reproduce an end-stop setting procedure.
- **Seals/drains:** PET 811-010 gives roof-frame/B-pillar/roof-arch seals; 811-012, 811-013 and 812-000 give drain plates, compartment hoses and side drain components. Diagrams are conceptual, not concealed-hose routing maps. Heating operation and windstop ventilation note: owner manual printed pp. 31 and 189. Roof and rubber-seal care: printed pp. 215 and 218.

## Important documented running change

Porsche Technical Information **29/09 ENU 5393**, *Cabriolet Top Compartment Water Drains*, applies to Boxster/Boxster S 987 as of MY2005. [Full reproduced primary bulletin](https://charm.li/Porsche/2011/Boxster%20%28987%29%20F6-2.9L/Repair%20and%20Diagnosis/Technical%20Service%20Bulletins/All%20Technical%20Service%20Bulletins/Body%20-%20Convertible%20Top%20Drain%20Information/).

- Leaves/debris can collect between top and compartment lid, enter when the roof is opened, block drains and cause interior water ingress.
- Additional rear-drain filters introduced **31 August 2009**; retrofit described for earlier cars.
- Filters do not remove the ongoing need to inspect and clean the compartment. This is a build-date distinction, not registration-year evidence.

The chapter uses the causal explanation and introduction date only. The booklet's service-position and fitting sequence are not reproduced.

## Catalogue options and other models

- **2010 UK choices:** ordinary brochure printed p. 102 lists Black, Stone Grey, Metropole Blue, Cocoa and Red hood colours. Printed p. 106 lists hardtop **550** and three-piece wind deflector **551**, available for ordinary Boxster and S. Printed p. 81 gives aluminium hardtop, 23 kg, heated rear window; wind deflector reduces head/shoulder turbulence. Read the table legend: both are options, not standard 2.9 equipment.
- **Hardtop accessory evidence:** Porsche *Tequipment PET 2010*, illustration 801-51, sheet dated **28 January 2010**, [scan pp. 194–195](https://www.9xxteile.com/media/wysiwyg/pet/E_TEQ_KATALOG.pdf#page=194), lists complete painted hardtop 987 563 005 00 for Boxster 987, “05–”, and points to installation instruction 6117. This is distinct from the dated factory sales option. Owner manual printed p. 190 states that rear pivot-lock inserts are absent on vehicles delivered without hardtop, requiring the necessary parts before first fitting. No current stock, colour availability or blanket Spyder/Black Edition fitment claim.
- **2009:** launch UK brochure edition 08/08, printed pp. 80–81 ([PDF](https://porschecarshistory.com/wp-content/old/brochures5/26/2009%20987%20Boxster%20UK.pdf#page=39)), supports the same broad ordinary roof claims. **2011–2012:** later PET supplies shared service-architecture coverage, not proof of every original suffix, supplier or annual colour/order-list choice.
- **Black Edition:** UK *Price List* 03/2011, printed p. **10** (scan p. 6), explicitly lists electrically operated hood with heated glass, 50 km/h operation, and three-part wind deflector. Original [UK price list](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/gtavw6els2231u61qjomr/2011-987-Porsche-Boxster-Black-Edition-Price-List-UK.pdf?rlkey=7n0i52owbntdg7d17dfgyo7nw&dl=0) was rendered locally because the copy is scanned. Black roof/marking independently established by [Porsche Netherlands release, 2 February 2011](https://nieuws.porsche.nl/slechts-987-exemplaren-rijk-uitgeruste-boxster-s-black-edition/).
- **Spyder:** UK brochure 01/10, printed pp. **20–21**, [scan p. 9](https://porschecarshistory.com/wp-content/old/brochures2/93/2010%20987%20Boxster%20Spyder.pdf#page=9): manual two-piece sunshield/weather protector, no control motors, 21 kg saving, rear-luggage-compartment forward stowage. **200 km/h / 124 mph with roof closed**, repeated for both transmissions in printed p. **41**, [scan p. 19](https://porschecarshistory.com/wp-content/old/brochures2/93/2010%20987%20Boxster%20Spyder.pdf#page=19). Distinct from the ordinary roof's 50 km/h operating ceiling. Photograph object 29 shows the sunshield with rear opening, not the complete enclosure.

## Unknowns and illustration limits

The inspected 2009 Technik introduction is a change-focused technical source, not the basis for a complete roof mechanism reconstruction. Use PET/owner manual/roof-specific WM instead. The evidence recovered here does not establish every roof end-switch location, exact control sequencing, travel calibration, drainage capacity or complete annual UK colour/option sequence. No workshop calibration or linkage geometry is invented. Later service commonality does not prove original assembly interchangeability.

The two interactive sequence/section diagrams use an ordinary 2010 roof, alongside the separate 3D explorer below. Opening stops separate overlapping real movements. Drainage has an expressly illustrative graphic overflow threshold; it is not a measured Boxster capacity/rainfall model. Their SVG posters and the two static SVG schematics explain the sequence, drive topology, water-management distinction and short-drop principle. Five real period photographs, extracted from the cited UK brochures, have local credits and visible manuscript source captions; their copyright is separate from this repository's MIT licence.

Evidence-led additions:
