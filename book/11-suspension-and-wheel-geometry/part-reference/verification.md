## Car-to-component tour and eight groups

The same IDs and the 0/30/65/100 guided stages are retained. **Location** identifies the front-left corner of standard 2010 2.9; **overview** traces the guidance, steering and anti-roll functions; **assembled** names the actual shared joints and front retaining stub; **exploded** reveals the bearing, seats, protective parts and carrier sensor while explicitly denying a removal sequence. **Focus** remains the coil-over. The standard passive model contains no PASM actuator, Spyder spring or PR 603 lighting-level sensor.

| Selectable group | Drawing-backed identity and retained approximation |
| --- | --- |
| `wheel` | Wheel-to-hub attachment and tyre-force role confirmed. Translucent five-spoke teaching wheel is intentionally illustrative; it is not a 2010 standard wheel/tyre fitment specification. |
| `carrier` | 401-005 carrier/bearing/hub/retaining bolt/nut/speed sensor. Added stub and nut; short sensor tail ends locally and does not claim vehicle wiring routing. Brake disc/caliper deliberately belong to chapter 13. |
| `coilover` | 402-000 damper, main coil, seats, bellows, auxiliary stop and separate front thrust bearing. Proportions, spring turns and compressed lengths are illustrative. All remain one selectable strut group. |
| `body-mounts` | Front body tower/top mount, crossmember, lower-arm/guide/rack/bar supporting references. Added three top-mount studs; disconnected reference geometry is not a removable complete subframe assembly. |
| `lower-arm` | 401-000 wishbone 6, inboard bush and outer ball joint; guide attaches partway along arm. Arm section and joint centres approximate. |
| `guide-link` | 401-000 guide 3: arm to forward body mounting. Not a tie rod or a second wishbone. |
| `steering-link` | 403-000 rack/inner joint/gaiter/locknut/outer track rod. Outer joint meets carrier steering ear; not a rear toe actuator. Steering rack housing support is represented schematically. |
| `anti-roll-bar` | 402-005 transverse bar, left drop link and end joints. Bar supported by bushes in body-reference group; left drop link meets strut bracket. Opposite link omitted in explicit single-corner view. |
