## Geometry decisions and limits

The old tall compartments, tiny wheel and bent-line seat have been replaced by a low curved rear-body section, a reclining upholstered integrated-headrest seat, rollover hoop and wheel of credible relative diameter. The folded roof and rear glass sit above the internal top-cover boundary; the engine is ahead of the rear axle, with a muted aft transaxle providing context. The rear boot has a shaped floor and separate lid, rather than continuing into the roof well. The oil/coolant service cup remains within the rear boot and is also shown as a larger face-view detail.

Carpet, engine cover and gasket are close together in the installed section. Only the explicitly labelled lower detail separates them, showing a formed cover with ribs/fastener locations and a continuous perimeter gasket. Layer selection changes emphasis without moving physical parts or implying a removal order. The four existing control labels, retained state, `aria-pressed`, `data-body-layer` and abort lifecycle are preserved. The checked-in poster is generated from the same all-spaces drawing and stays useful without scripting.

Curves, section placement, cover thicknesses, rib/fastener counts, folding geometry, engine detail and wheel spokes are approximate teaching geometry, visually informed by the named references. The boot capacity is Porsche's specification, not a volume calculated from this section. The rigid rear glass/fabric stack is a stowage illustration, not a kinematic roof simulation. No calibrated dimensions, precise mounting coordinates, steel-gauge claims or workshop procedure have been inferred.
