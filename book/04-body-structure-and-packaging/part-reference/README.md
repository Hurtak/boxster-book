## Page inventory and applicability

All page numbers below are **1-based original PDF pages**; the plate's internal leaf number is a different number. Every saved PNG is linked. Each row names the component callouts used for this chapter, rather than transcribing all fasteners. The whole PDF remains the authority for omitted table leaves and VIN/equipment conditions.

Generic shell/closure entries without an engine filter cover the listed body model life; that does not prove an unchanged original part suffix for each year or exchangeability of complete assemblies. **A120** is the 2.9 engine context; **PR:099** identifies RHD and **PR:098** LHD where printed. Code-key pages 8–14 identify **PR:480** as six-speed manual, **PR:250** as PDK, **PR:599** as aluminium doors, **PR:778** as Spyder, and **XAL** as the Aerokit. Manual/PDK bumper branches remain distinct; the legacy PR:249 Tiptronic label elsewhere in PET does not put Tiptronic into the 2010 baseline. The later replacement-parts snapshot cannot establish original 2010 option availability or introduction chronology.

## What the drawings establish

The pages establish named service parts, handedness and assembly relationships: formed floor and seat supports, inner sill reinforcement and outer skin, layered scuttle/A-pillar pieces, covers versus bumper carriers, luggage-lid hardware, handed side-air assemblies, divided underbody panels and separate engine/roof/luggage access covers. Their exploded spacing is an identification device. It is not installed clearance, a production seam map, body-repair cutting guidance, a workshop removal order or a calibrated motion model.

The January 2010 UK brochure remains the evidence for luggage capacity, aluminium luggage lids, ordinary roof packaging, material categories and original equipment. Porsche's MY2010 handbook remains the evidence for automatic spoiler thresholds and warnings. Porsche training supplies structural/airflow principles. Exact locators and existing source links are retained in [research.md](../research.md) and the chapter's original endnotes; no new endnote IDs were added.

Exact steel grades, gauge, weld pattern, mounting coordinates, structural stiffness, crash performance, CFD streamlines, original part supersessions, annual UK body revisions and complete interchangeability remain unestablished. The chapter's original geometry stays explicitly illustrative. The shared appearance car's omitted rear spoiler is an intentional presentation choice; the real assembly is documented above without modifying that shared asset.
