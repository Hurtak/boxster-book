## Corrections and useful detail

- Body length/width is approximately **2.411** and wheelbase/body length approximately **0.556**, instead of the former short, wide trapezoid. At the drawing's nominal **6 mm per SVG unit**, the body is approximately **724 × 300 units** and axles **402.5 units** apart. Front and rear tyre envelopes are approximately **109.6 × 34.2 / 111.1 × 39.2 units**. Their fore/aft body offset remains visually guided, not a factory overhang measurement.
- Curved wings, bonnet seam, teardrop headlamps, mirrors, curved windscreen, two separate seats, centre console, transparent roof/deck region and rear luggage lid make the ordinary 987.2 arrangement recognisable. These details are context, not new claims about detachable parts or materials.
- The engine occupies the region **behind the cabin and ahead of the rear axle**; rear luggage remains farther aft. Its paired bank/casting features and the rearward transmission context replace a generic engine rectangle. Casting profiles and intake geometry remain simplified.
- Each front module has a flared air-guide footprint, two separate thin cores, rear shroud/fan viewed edge-on, and hose connections. The side cutaway distinguishes **condenser → coolant radiator → rear fan**. The condenser's refrigerant circuit is not connected to the coolant system.
- The vehicle-left opening feeds a moulded airbox and intake connection; the vehicle-right opening feeds a separate duct/fan and arrows into the surrounding engine bay. Fan blades are not drawn lying flat in the plan view. Numbers and explanatory sentences preserve the three jobs without relying on colour alone.
