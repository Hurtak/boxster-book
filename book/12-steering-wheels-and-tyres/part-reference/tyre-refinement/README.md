# Tyre cross-section reference check

Inspected 14 November 2026. The manuscript drawing is original project work, not a traced manufacturer illustration or an exact 2010 Porsche tyre reconstruction.

## Sources retained unchanged

- **Continental**, [Tyre components](https://www.continental-tires.com/au/en/tyre-knowledge/tire-components/), tread/belt/casing/bead sections. [Original PNG](https://www.continental-tires.com/au/en/tyre-knowledge/tire-components/_jcr_content/root/container/container_772073047/container/image.coreimg.png/1659691059279/co-tirebasics-tirecomponents-graphic-en-plt.png), `continental-construction-original.png`. Stepped cutaway establishes layer order; text explains cap ply, steel belts, textile casing, liner, sidewall, bead apex and wire core. Its numbered labels are the manufacturer's own, not the manuscript key. The separated layers are not a dimensional section.
- **Hankook**, [Tyre Structure](https://www.hankooktire.com/uk/en/help-support/tire-guide/tire-structure.html), [original full-resolution image](https://asset.hankooktire.com/content/dam/hankooktire/local/img/help-support/tire-guide/tire-structure/Tire%20Structure_w.jpg), `hankook-cutaway-original.jpg`. Visual check of broad crown, rounded shoulders, flexible sidewalls, belt package and bead. The photograph/technical render names a **Ventus S1 evo3 SUV, 285/45 R19**; it is construction evidence only, not the reference car's tyre, supplier, tread pattern or dimensions.
- **Porsche**, The Boxster, UK 01/10, printed pp. 126–127, confirms standard front **205/55 ZR17 on 7J×17**. See the chapter's existing brochure/handbook references. Nominal section ratio in the new drawing is **220 / 400 = 0.55**. The inner flange spacing is about **347 SVG units**, approximately **177.8 / 205 × 400**. These proportions guide the generic outline; they do not establish actual tyre width, tread width, loaded shape, rim-flange contour or material thickness.

## Refinement and limits

The outer casing and cavity now have rounded shoulders and curved sidewalls instead of straight vertical walls. Two belts and a thin cap band follow the crown, the casing turns around the bead, seven stylised wire sections identify each core, and a rim flange/seat/drop centre replaces the solid horizontal bar. Tread grooves are shown only in the rubber; none cuts into the belts. Cord count, cap-ply specification, thickness, bead filler, flange radius and loaded deformation are illustrative.

Five leaders start on their numbered circle's perimeter and end with an SVG arrowhead, whose `refX` puts its tip at the target coordinate. All target points are on their named material path; no floating end dots remain. Native HTML supplies the label text and key, so phone readers do not depend on tiny words inside the image. No source photograph is composited or annotated into a new bitmap.
