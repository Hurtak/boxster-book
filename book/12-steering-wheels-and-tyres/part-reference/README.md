## Interpretation

The reference remains the **2010 European/UK 2.9 with six-speed manual and standard 17-inch wheels**. PDK, larger wheels and TPM are alternatives. PR098/099 identify steering handedness; PR480/250 identify manual/PDK; PR394/399 identify the standard/alternative 17-inch wheel families. The catalogue's model overview identifies A120 as the 2.9 and A121 as the 3.4. Lists without an engine restriction support the shared component relationship, not complete interchangeability.

PET proves installed assembly identity, separate parts and listed applications. It does not establish exact coordinates, hydraulic valve internals, steering ratio curves, load transfer, tyre forces, rolling circumference, original market orderability, or workshop removal order. The chapter's hydraulic cutaway follows Bosch's generic operating explanation; tyre construction and combined grip follow the existing manufacturer sources. The drawings here do not supply tyre construction or operating-force evidence.
