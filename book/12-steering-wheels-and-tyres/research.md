## Tyre principles

- Continental, [Tyre components](https://www.continental-tires.com/au/en/tyre-knowledge/tire-components/), opened and read: tread/belt versus casing, radial ply, inner liner, sidewall and bead functions. Generic tyre mechanism, not a claim about the exact tyre supplier fitted in 2010.
- Michelin, [What can cause excessive or uneven tyre wear?](https://www.michelin.co.uk/auto/advice/change-tyres/tyre-wear), updated 22 July 2025, opened and read: underinflation, flexing/heat, pressure/wear, cold checks. No pressure value imported from a generic tyre site.
- Contact-patch discussion is qualitative. The chapter explicitly excludes an exact area = load/pressure prediction; carcass forces and nonuniform contact pressure matter.

## Original diagrams and numerical contract

1. `images/steering-hydraulic.svg`: conceptual driver/shaft/pinion/rack/track-rod path plus pump/reservoir assistance circuit. Not a hose-routing map, measured rack or replacement-parts assembly drawing.
2. `images/wheel-offset.svg`: centre plane, nominal width between flange faces and positive mounting-face offset. The equal-width ET55-to-ET45 comparison moves the rim out by 10 mm; illustrative alteration only.
3. `diagrams/tyres-rolling-size/`: useful native-button/slider D3 comparison, with `poster.svg`. Initial state is rear tyres, comparison 18-inch, road speed 60 mph. Rims/tyres are drawn to a common geometric scale. Input labels omit Z solely to shorten illustration text; manuscript retains Porsche's ZR notation.

Diameter = rim inches × 25.4 + 2 × width mm × aspect ÷ 100. Circumference = π × diameter. Wheel rpm = mph × 1609.344 ÷ 60 ÷ circumference metres. These are nominal geometric estimates, explicitly not loaded circumference, a speedometer calibration or an approval tool.

## 3D steering and front-wheel explorer

`diagrams/steering-explorer/` adds eight selectable groups: steering wheel/column, rack and hydraulic assistance, engine-driven pump/reservoir, supply/return paths, left and right track-rod/carrier/hub groups, and separate standard front rim and tyre. The column/pinion/rack/track-rod relationship follows Porsche PET illustration 403-000; the original drawing on PDF p. 138 was rendered and visually inspected. Fresh online Porsche text also confirms the separate track rods and bellows. PET 101-010 (PDF pp. 37–38) identifies the belt and power-steering pump pulley; 403-001 and 403-003 identify the supply/return paths, pump and reservoir. [Porsche PET](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf#page=138).

The fresh UK 01/10 brochure text, printed pp. 51 and 126–127, independently confirms hydraulic assistance, mechanical variable ratio and the standard front combination. [Porsche brochure](https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf). The rim uses nominal 7-inch width between the inner flange faces and 17-inch bead-seat diameter; the unladen tyre uses the 205/55 nominal geometry. The five-hole mounting pattern is consistent with the evidence already recorded above. No load radius, pressure, footprint, steering ratio curve or tyre supplier is inferred.

Installed locations are schematic: the gear lies across the front axle region; the column rises into the left-hand driving position; hydraulic paths cross the car from the rear power unit. The cutaway rack piston, steering arms, joints, belt segment, pump mount, spokes, tread pattern and line bends explain relationships rather than factory geometry. The cooler, brake and suspension arms are omitted explicitly. Explosion offsets are display separation, not steering travel or a removal procedure. Parent integration supplies the rendered poster and shared browser/phone/offline checks.
