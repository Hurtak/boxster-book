# Chapter 15: factory-drawing verification

Audit: **10 November 2026**. Scope: the complete chapter manuscript, three standalone explanatory SVGs, two interactive figures and both SVG fallbacks, photographs/captions, and the chapter-owned car-to-cabin model. The [reference inventory](README.md) links all **67 saved full factory pages**, including **29 visually inspected drawings**, with application tables. The fixed subject remains the **2010 European/UK 2.9**, standard manual, ordinary standard seats and manually controlled A/C. PDK, electric/memory seats, heating, ventilation, automatic climate and special models remain explicit alternatives.

“Confirmed” below means the stated identity or relationship is supported; it does not turn a schematic into factory CAD. “Illustrative” identifies a deliberate teaching abstraction. No unsupported installation coordinate, adjustment travel, flap percentage, thermal performance or automatic motion has been added.

## Photographs and limits of this pass

All three existing photographs remain credited to Porsche in [image credits](../images/credits.md). They are source examples rather than technical drawings: comfort/memory PDK cabin is optioned; aluminium-dial dashboard is an optioned S; separate bucket picture is the optional folding bucket. PET did not justify altering those captions or treating visible leather/perforations as proof of hidden equipment.

ManualsLib training pages159/160 did not open in the fresh web check. Porsche-authored training text at the existing Scribd source was accessible and freshly checked for151–154; this pass does not claim to have saved a fresh image of its fan illustration. The factory PET pages are actually saved and inspected, and all fan-count/direction claims remain tied to the accessible training text rather than guessed from PET's abbreviated English labels.

Local checks cover JavaScript syntax, all SVGs as XML, exact manuscript heading/footnote sequence, finite geometry/normals, named parts/connection endpoints and a car-sized installed envelope. No shared runtime/build edit, browser build, poster generation, staging or commit was performed by the chapter agent. Parent regenerated **cabin-explorer** and visually checked the longer **cabin-heat-paths.svg** with the other current chapter SVGs; full-reader checks are recorded in the central integration review.
