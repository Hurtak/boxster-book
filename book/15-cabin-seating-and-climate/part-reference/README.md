## Application conventions and limits

- A120 is the 2.9 engine family; A121 is the 3.4. HVAC and seat lists often apply by equipment rather than by engine. A `997` or `996` part prefix alone proves neither an identical original complete assembly nor interchangeability.
- PR098 = LHD, PR099 = RHD, PR480 = six-speed manual, PR250 = PDK. Standard seat369/370, sports375/376, electric sports377/378, comfort437/438, bucket388/389 are left/right component families; memory537/538, heating342 and ventilation541 are distinct qualifications. These are component application codes, not replacements for sales packages P15/P77/P01/P03.
- PR572 is manual A/C; PR573 is automatic A/C in this chapter's dated UK equipment context; PR574 is no A/C. PR541 entries do not prove that every leather seat is ventilated. PR560 occupant recognition is a separate market/equipment distinction.
- Parts drawings establish assembly identity, major fasteners, mounting relationships and separate pipes/ducts. They do **not** establish measured coordinates, material, physical motion, forces, airflow percentages, control calibration, removal order, original 2010 orderability or a complete annual change history. A later common replacement/supersession is not proof that all original units were identical.
- Generic catalogue graphics are reused: the control-panel graphic does not establish all 2010 switch legends; a compressor pulley does not prove a magnetic clutch; a single drawn condenser is listed as **quantity two**. Dated brochure, handbook and Porsche training remain the authorities for options and operation.
- PET's English term “Airbag” for PR541 air-distribution inserts and lumbar air elements must not be mistaken for the separately listed **side airbag unit**. Ventilation direction and cushion/backrest fan modules are established by Porsche training printed152–154, not inferred from that translation.

## Operating principles and original equipment

[Chapter research](../research.md) retains exact dated brochure/handbook/training locators. In particular, UK **01/10** brochure printed74–76 and110–111 controls the seat/climate options; **PNA 987 021 09**, 2/09, printed145–154 explains clutchless compressor control and seat ventilation; **WKD 987 021 10**, 5/09, explains adjustment, recirculation and demisting. [DENSO receiver-drier explanation](https://www.denso-am.eu/products/ac-engine-cooling/receiver-drier) supplies general liquid-storage/filtering/drying function, not proof of a particular Porsche replacement part. No complete ordinary-model UK 2011–2012 ordering sequence has been established.
