# Seat ventilation refinement

Checked **14 November 2026**. The reference is the 2010 European/UK Boxster2.9 with optional **PR541** ventilation on an eligible standard/comfort seat, not its standard Alcantara upholstery. No ventilation is assigned to the sports bucket seat.

## Drawing changes and limits

The former short L-shaped slab is replaced by a taller seat with an integrated head restraint, rearward recline and raised front cushion. Bolsters and most of the frame are cut away. The backrest intake is now correctly on the occupant-facing side, which is the right-hand side of the backrest in this view. Cushion intake is downward from the top. Each branch connects to its own fan; the fans are no longer unattached three-blade symbols or connected to one shared continuous L-shaped pipe.

The second panel enlarges the perforated cover, open spacer mesh and foam passages, ending at an inlet-face view of the cushion blower. Its annular inlet surrounds a solid central motor cap; it is not a large open propeller. The visible housing/motor proportions, tabs and seal follow the part photographs. Main-view modules are profile symbols, while the enlarged module is face-on. The connecting duct is unfolded and the exhaust outlet is placed for explanation; no exact moulded duct, mounting angle, blade count, contour dimensions, foam/mat thickness or measured airflow is claimed. Heating elements, upholstery attachments and most structural parts are omitted from this ventilation-only view.

Numbers are native React overlays, with one shared text key. Only blue arrows denote air movement; there are no label leaders. `markers.json` records image-relative percentage coordinates. The seat outline is photo-guided, not a measured seat drawing. Actual backrest adjustment and the concealed geometry vary by seat/version.
