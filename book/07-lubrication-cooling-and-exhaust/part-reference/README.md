# Chapter 07 factory parts references

Checked 10 November 2026 for the **2010 European/UK Boxster 987.2 2.9, six-speed manual**, with PDK and sports exhaust treated as options. These original factory drawings support component identity, attachments and plumbing. Operating principles are separately documented in [research.md](../research.md); the complete asset and topic audit is in [verification.md](verification.md).

## Evidence boundaries

These are later service-parts records. A shared row can identify a shared listed part, but does not prove that complete assemblies are interchangeable or that every MY2010 car left the factory with that replacement revision. The original 2010 UK brochure controls standard/optional equipment; the 2009 Porsche training manual controls oil-pump regulation, cross-flow head cooling and catalyst-brick/sensor sequence. PET's “before/after catalytic converter” labels are less specific than the training account of two bricks inside each manifold-integrated converter.

Exploded placement is not installed CAD or removal order. The book's dimensions, pump gears, vanes, spring, plate stack, hoses, fan blades, heat shields and separation offsets remain educational approximations. Centre-radiator applicability, exact thermostat/fan calibrations and complete annual production breakpoints remain unresolved. No exact fluid pressures, temperatures, material specifications, repair instructions or failure rates are derived from these drawings.
