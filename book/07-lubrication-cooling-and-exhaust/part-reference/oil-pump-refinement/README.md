# Oil-pump illustration references — 14 November 2026

Reference: 2010 European/UK Boxster 987.2 2.9. Original files are retained byte-for-byte; captions, layout and annotations are supplied separately by React or authored SVG. No photograph was composited, repainted or generatively modified.

## Evidence

- [Wolf Auto Parts PS85365](https://wolfautoparts.com/parts/engine-oil-pump-9a110705004-ps85365/): three photographs of the actual **9A1 107 050 04** assembly. They show a compact multi-section casting, a drive sprocket, oil openings, mounting bosses and a slender separate control valve. The number matches a pump listed for A120/A121 in [PET 104-000, PDF pp. 55–56](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf#page=55), not proof of every car's original suffix or wholesale interchangeability. The first photograph is displayed in the book.
- [Porsche, 2009 Boxster F6-2.9, Oil supply](https://charm.li/Porsche/2009/Boxster%20%28987%29%20F6-2.9L/Repair%20and%20Diagnosis/Engine%2C%20Cooling%20and%20Exhaust/Engine/Description%20and%20Operation/Oil%20Supply/): original workshop description and illustrations, independently hosted by Operation CHARM. The oil-pan cutaway locates the complete assembly and exposes the pressure section and spring. The functional gear/piston drawing identifies the driven fixed gear, axially movable gear, spring and control valve. Both illustrations were visually inspected. The oil-pan view is displayed in the book; the functional drawing is retained as reference evidence for the authored section.
- Porsche *2009 Technik Introduction*, **PNA 987 021 09, edition 2/09**, Engine printed **pp. 21–22**, [indexed original text](https://www.scribd.com/document/972719474/Boxster-2009): mechanism authority. The pressure difference controls axial position and engaged tooth width; the crankshaft supplies mechanical drive. The four extraction sections remain distinct from this pressure section. No numerical flow, pressure or travel is derived.
- Existing [PET drawing](../pet-104-000-p055.png) and [parts list](../pet-104-000-p056.png): **987209/P33, 19 July 2018**, pump item **1**, sprocket **8**, separate chain **10**, control valve **17**. ## Drawing decisions and limits

The old long, thin rectangular gears are replaced by short cylindrical spur-gear sections with teeth running along their axes. Relative gear diameter, axial width, piston length and spring envelope follow the factory mechanism drawing. The complete casting and valve photograph supplies physical context; it cannot reveal internal gear dimensions. The pressure section is enlarged and its hydraulic connections unfolded, so its outer rectangle is not a silhouette of the complete pump.

The lower gear, its pistons and spring seat translate together while the upper gear remains in position. The blue band and bracket span the intersection of the two gear widths. Reduced overlap shortens that band without stopping either gear. The spring changes compression. Tooth count, clearances, galleries, bore dimensions, drive ratio, spring rate, valve port positions, current and axial travel remain illustrative. Valve energisation is not mapped to these two non-numerical teaching positions.

Box labels use shared geometric centres and centred SVG baselines. The explicit double-headed movement arrow and its label share the sliding gear's centre in each state, rather than being positioned independently. Leader endpoints remain on the named piston, gear and spring.
