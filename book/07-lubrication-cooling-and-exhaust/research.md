# Chapter 07 research: lubrication, cooling and exhaust

Checked 10 November 2026. Reference: European/UK model-year 2010 Boxster 987.2 2.9, standard manual transmission. Contemporary Porsche documents govern the model-specific account. Later PET evidence is used for component relationships and identification, without claiming that a later replacement listing proves every original part was identical.

## Remaining limits

A complete original UK cooling configuration matrix and annual part-breakpoint history have not been reconstructed. The chapter therefore avoids unconditional centre-radiator fitment claims and exact thermostat/fan calibrations. PET establishes later documented hardware, while the Cayman sports-exhaust instruction is explicitly bounded comparison evidence. No failure rates, service intervals or repair procedures are supplied.
