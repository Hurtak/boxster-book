# Factory electrical parts references

Porsche-authored **PET 987209**, *Model life 2009>>2012*, catalogue **P33**, snapshot **19 July 2018**, 583 PDF pages. Independently hosted [source PDF](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf); retrieved and checked **10 November 2026**.

Source PDF SHA-256: `c865de9263f5946db4c24874eb10e503be2343762b308695aa7bb6a943bfd2bf`.

The PNGs are actual full factory pages, including numbered callouts, page furniture, date and adjacent application lists, rendered with Poppler at 1,800 pixels on the long edge. No drawing was redrawn, cropped, annotated or generated. Page numbers below are **one-based PDF pages**, not the plate-local `-1`, `-2` labels. Every selected drawing was visually inspected against the chapter. [page-manifest.json](page-manifest.json) provides the same file/page mapping for tooling.

Copyright remains with Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG; the project's MIT licence does not relicense these excerpts. The independent host is not credited as the drawing's author. These excerpts support the requested personal reference draft; they do not duplicate the full catalogue.

## Files and applications

The reference car remains the **2010 European/UK 2.9 (A120), six-speed manual (PR 480)**; **PDK (250)** is an option. PR **098/099** means LHD/RHD, whereas **492** means headlights for **left-hand traffic**. These are different selection criteria. PR **465/466** separately identifies the rear fog lamp's side. A brochure package such as **P75** is not interchangeable with an individual component's PET PR code.

## How the evidence is used

Use a drawing together with its parts/application rows. An exploded view identifies assemblies, mounting relationships and separately supplied components. It is not a wiring diagram, a harness-routing measurement, a photometric drawing, a workshop removal order or proof of current retail supply. The snapshot includes supersessions and discontinued entries; shared replacement numbers do not establish identical original equipment or complete interchangeability.

The model's alternator and starter internals, lamp optical surfaces, circuit arrows, rail-like body continuity and separation offsets remain teaching geometry. PET does not supply their internal mechanism, calibration, forces, timing or precise installed coordinates. The standard manual flywheel-drive teaching example does not specify PDK's internal starter-ring geometry.

Original **UK 01/10 Porsche sales brochure** printed pp. 64–65, 77–79, 106 and 110 defines standard halogen/fog/LED-position lights, optional **bi-xenon headlights with dynamic cornering lights**, P75/P12 and alarm/tracking packages. Its broad p. 17 DRL wording conflicts with the detailed lighting section; the chapter retains the detailed distinction and country qualifier. No later “PDLS” branding is introduced. The source is linked in [research.md](../research.md), together with the North American MY2010 handbook's explicit market limits and the separately dated Spyder/Black Edition sources.
