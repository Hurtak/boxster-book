# Chapter 17 factory parts references

Checked **10 November 2026** for the **2010 European/UK Boxster 987.2 2.9**. The standard CDR-30/four-speaker installation is the explorer's subject; PCM, audio upgrades and telephone equipment remain explicit options.

The source's PR key is generic across applications. Here **098/099** distinguishes LHD/RHD, **441** the basic radio-speaker system, **490** Sound Package Plus (PET calls it ASK), **665** PCM, **680** BOSE, **695** CDR-30, and **870** the multimedia interface. Dated UK brochures, rather than this later key, establish the marketed packages and dependencies. Japan-specific PR675 equipment is not applied to the UK reference car.

## What the drawings do and do not establish

The pages establish separately catalogued assemblies, mounting identities, model/equipment restrictions and named connections. They do not supply measured component positions, installation order, electrical pinouts, exact signal processing, speaker construction sections, Bluetooth profiles, current device support or first retail availability. In particular:

- The radio signal amplifier is distinct from an audio power amplifier. Radio, GPS and telephone reception equipment must not be merged into one aerial merely because they occupy the same catalogue section.
- The eleven-speaker BOSE count comes from the period brochure/training, not from counting PET assembly rows. The rear enclosure includes more than one transducer.
- The separately drawn telephone service module does not overturn the period training's integrated PCM architecture. It is not evidence that every listed module box is mounted separately in every configuration.
- The antenna pattern and component locations in the explorer remain illustrative. PR440 diversity hardware is not silently assigned to the baseline car.
- Replacement dates, supersessions and exchange parts are not evidence of new 2010 UK retail options, streaming support or an unchanged 2009–2012 software history.
