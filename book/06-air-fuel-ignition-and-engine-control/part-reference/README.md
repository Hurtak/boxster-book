# Chapter 06 factory parts references

Checked 10 November 2026 for the **2010 European/UK Boxster 987.2 2.9**, standard six-speed manual and optional seven-speed PDK.

## Source and preservation

Porsche PET, **model 987209, model life 2009–2012**, catalogue snapshot **19 July 2018**, **Kat P33**, 583 PDF pages. [Original Porsche-authored catalogue, independently hosted](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf). The catalogue identifies **A120** as the 2.9 engine and **A121** as the 3.4; the PR key identifies **480** as manual, **250** as PDK, **098** as left-hand drive and **099** as right-hand drive.

Original PDF SHA-256: `c865de9263f5946db4c24874eb10e503be2343762b308695aa7bb6a943bfd2bf`.

The 36 PNGs below are unaltered full-page Poppler renderings, 1,800 pixels on the long edge, preserving Porsche headers, illustration numbers, date, numbered callouts and footer. They are supporting reference assets, not original project artwork; original rights remain Porsche's and the repository MIT licence does not relicense them. The complete PDF is not duplicated here. Every saved drawing was visually inspected, and its relevant item identities/applicability were read in the adjacent parts text. PDF page numbers below are **one-based physical pages**, not the plate's internal `-1/-2` numbering.

## How to read this evidence

PET supports component identity, quantity, visible joints, assembly neighbours and the listed engine/PR distinctions. The [chapter research](../research.md) separately cites Porsche's period technical introduction for approximately 4-bar port injection, 40–120-bar DFI, flap operating thresholds, active ignition, sensor purpose, catalyst-brick sequence and emissions strategies; Bosch/HELLA explain generic mechanisms. This replacement-parts snapshot establishes neither original 2010 option availability nor a complete annual parts/software history. Exploded drawing offsets do not establish installation coordinates, removal order, movement, internal fluid/gas paths, material or control calibration. Geometry in the reader remains an educational approximation.
