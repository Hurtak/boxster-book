# Intake shape references — 14 November 2026

Reference car: European/UK 2010 Boxster 987.2 2.9. These files inform the visible proportions of the `intake-resonance-states` illustration. They are original downloaded JPEGs, retained byte-for-byte. The manuscript imports the complete-assembly view 2 and centre-distributor view 1 separately through the shared React comparison layout; no labels, composites or generative edits are baked into the photographs.

## Identity and applicability

- **Complete assembly:** [Viking Auto Salvage item 112870](https://www.vikingusedautoparts.com/part/112870), stock AA0982, listed donor 2010 Cayman 2.9. This is a related 2.9 assembly photograph, not evidence of a particular UK Boxster's equipment. The seller's free-text number list contains `94111002022`; that string is not treated as a verified Porsche part number.
- **Bank distributor:** [Wolf Auto Parts PS85570](https://wolfautoparts.com/intake-manifold-air-distributor-9a111011522-ps85570.html), listed **9A1 110 115 22**. This matches PET 107-010 item 1's A120 application. Three curved runners, the elongated chamber, mounting bosses and flange shape are visible. No S/A121 runner dimensions are transferred.
- **Centre distributor:** [Wolf Auto Parts PS85425](https://wolfautoparts.com/parts/air-distribution-tube-9a111012022-ps85425/), listed **9A1 110 120 22** as a later replacement for **9A1 110 020 22**. This listing establishes the photographed object's identity as sold, not the original 2010 suffix or complete interchangeability. The broad ribbed envelope, divided oval opening and throttle neck agree with the complete assembly and factory drawing.
- **Factory exploded drawing:** Porsche workshop **WM 244619**, 2010 Cayman F6-2.9, [intake-manifold service reproduction](https://charm.li/Porsche/2010/Cayman%20%28987%29%20F6-2.9L/Repair%20and%20Diagnosis/Engine%2C%20Cooling%20and%20Exhaust/Engine/Intake%20Manifold/Service%20and%20Repair/). Items 6/7/12 identify the centre resonance tube, throttle body and paired vacuum units. The original drawing was visually inspected; the adjacent torque tables are not represented as part illustrations.

The chapter's existing [PET 107-010 drawing](../pet-107-010-p92.png) and [item list](../pet-107-010-p93.png) were independently viewed. Catalogue: Porsche PET **987209, Kat P33, 19 July 2018**, [PDF pp. 92–93](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf#page=92), PDF SHA-256 `c865de9263f5946db4c24874eb10e503be2343762b308695aa7bb6a943bfd2bf`. A120 item 1 is **9A1 110 115 22** (two bank distributors); A121's **9A1 110 115 02** is a different application. Items 4/5/6 are the clamp/sleeve/clamp joints; item 8 the **9A1 110 020 22** centre assembly; item 10 throttle; items 12/13 the two change-over valves and two vacuum units. Exploded spacing does not measure installed gaps.

## Shape and mechanism decisions

The old rectangular banks and thin unfolded crossover bars are replaced with curved three-runner modules, elongated chambers, a broad two-lobed centre moulding with reinforcement webs, oval clamped joints, a throttle attachment neck, mounting bosses and both vacuum units/change-over valves. The drawing keeps these outer shapes identical in all three switching states. Numbered leaders end on visible components, not invented hidden flap coordinates.

The separate enlarged sections show half-oval flaps and the difference between a closed plate and an edge-on open plate. They are teaching sections, not a mapped bore layout, dimensions, actual shaft angles, vacuum plumbing or pressure-wave simulation. Photographs and exploded drawings do not provide the measurements needed to claim exact CAD proportions or installation coordinates.

Primary operation source: Porsche AfterSales Training, *2009 Technik Introduction — All Boxster/Cayman Models*, **PNA 987 021 09, 2/09**, Fuel/ignition **printed p. 59** for the 2.9, [indexed original text](https://www.scribd.com/document/972719474/Boxster-2009). Both flaps are vacuum-closed at idle; the distribution actuator is vented/open above **3,800 rpm**, the tuning actuator above **5,500 rpm**. Printed pp. 51–52 describe the combined centre housing, oval sections, half-oval flaps and inlet partition in the new intake design; the S-specific **5,300 rpm** threshold there is not used for the 2.9. Only text from the indexed reproduction was read; no fresh visual inspection of those training pages is claimed.
