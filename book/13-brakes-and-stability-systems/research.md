# Brake hardware source checks

Checked 10 November 2026. Ancillary research for chapter 13; the UK/European model-year 2010 Boxster 2.9 remains the reference. Original Porsche documents hosted by collectors are distinguished from official Porsche-hosted documents below. These are concise findings, not a complete annual parts audit.

## 2010 reference and 2009 introduction

**Porsche, _2009 Technik Introduction: All Boxster/Cayman Models_, printed p.119 (scan/HTML page 125):**

| Standard iron brakes | Front, diameter × thickness | Rear, diameter × thickness | Caliper finish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boxster 2.9 / Cayman 2.9 | 318 × 28 mm | 299 × 20 mm | Black anodised |
| Boxster S / Cayman S | 318 × 28 mm | 299 × 24 mm | Red paint |

The basic models' front discs increased from 298 × 24 mm on their predecessors. Porsche explicitly describes the new base front system as the S system. The S rear discs alone are 4 mm thicker. Both use drilled, internally ventilated discs. The document identifies standard discs as grey cast iron; standard-system booster ratio becomes 5, versus 4.5 with PCCB. Yellow calipers identify factory PCCB in this document. These are design/application statements, not proof that every complete assembly interchanges. [Original Porsche training document, printed p.119](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3550487/Porsche-Boxster-2009.html?page=125).

The same document calls ABS, ABD, ASR, MSR, EBD and wheel-specific dynamic control PSM functions. Pre-filling, brake assist and Drive-Off Assistant are described as enhanced longitudinal-control additions. [Printed p.120 / scan page 126](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3550487/Porsche-Boxster-2009.html?page=126).

**Porsche, _The Boxster_, UK edition 01/10, WSLB1101000125 GB/UK:** standard Boxster and S calipers are four-piston aluminium monobloc fixed units at both axles; disc diameters are 318/299 mm. Boxster calipers are black anodised, S red. The brake booster reduces pedal effort; front openings and ducts cool the wheel brakes. The technical table names PSM with ABS 8.0. [Printed pp.61 and 126–127](https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf#page=28).

PCCB is an S option, code 450; the ordinary 2.9 has a dash in the option matrix. PCCB has 350 mm ceramic discs front/rear, six front pistons and four rear pistons, aluminium monobloc fixed calipers. Porsche describes its discs as roughly half the mass of comparable conventional discs; this is not a whole-system weight reduction. Its track-use caveat requests professional brake inspection after each event. [Printed pp.62 and 107](https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf#page=29).

**Editorial trap:** the short brochure statement about S discs being 4 mm thicker must be read with the explicit axle-by-axle training dimensions above; do not make the S front disc 32 mm thick.

## Spyder and Black Edition

**Porsche, _The Boxster Spyder_, UK edition 01/10, WSLB1101000225 GB/UK:** PCCB is optional, and the standard brakes are four-piston aluminium monobloc fixed calipers with drilled, internally ventilated discs. PSM includes ABS 8.0. [Printed pp.31 and 42–43](https://porschecarshistory.com/wp-content/old/brochures2/93/2010%20987%20Boxster%20Spyder.pdf#page=14).

**Porsche, _Boxster Spyder Preisliste_, Germany, 08/2010, WPLB1101000411:** the standard hardware is red, four-piston fixed-caliper brakes, 318 × 28 mm front and 299 × 24 mm rear, with pad-wear monitoring. PCCB 450 has drilled, ventilated, carbon-fibre-reinforced ceramic discs, 350 mm at both axles, yellow calipers, six pistons front/four rear. This directly verifies S-sized steel disc dimensions on the Spyder; it does not prove identical part numbers. [Printed pp.8–11, 14–15 and 48–49](https://auto-preisliste.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/2010-08_preisliste_porsche_boxster-spyder.pdf).

**Porsche France, 2 February 2011:** PCCB is an optional extra on the Boxster S Black Edition. This verifies European availability, not the precise UK order-guide dependencies. [Original Porsche release](https://www.porsche.com/france/aboutporsche/pressreleases/pof/?id=2011-02-02&pool=france).

**Porsche Cars North America, 2012 technical sheet, p.1:** all four derivatives have four-piston aluminium monobloc fixed calipers and drilled, internally ventilated standard discs. Base calipers are black; S, Black Edition and Spyder are red. It lists rounded 12.5/11.8-inch front/rear diameters for each. Use this as US corroboration; do not silently convert the rounded figures into new European dimensions. [Official 2012 sheet](https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr:f533d37f-82ef-4c9c-90cc-56b43185a8de/2012_Boxster_Specs.pdf#page=1).

The same sheet has a plainly suspect p.2 row labelled “Wheels” containing six-piston brake data and 14.15/12.99-inch discs, repeated even for the base model. Do not use that row to establish PCCB specification or availability. It conflicts with the clear contemporary UK PCCB description above.

## Annual continuity and limits

The official US 2010 technical sheet, p.2, corroborates four-piston aluminium fixed monobloc calipers, drilled/ventilated discs and 318/299 mm diameters on Boxster/S. [Official 2010 sheet](https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr:e5abc517-c680-47c0-974f-b84aefde568f/2010-Boxster-Specifications.pdf#page=2).

The US 2010/11 sheet, p.2, repeats that ordinary-model brake description and those diameters. Its Spyder column is incompletely populated, so use the separate Spyder documents above. [Official 2010/11 sheet](https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr:48fa70bf-243a-4223-ad30-348614762824/2010-11_Boxster_Spyder_Specs.pdf#page=2).

These sources support the documented 2009 introduction and broad later hardware continuity. **No sourced annual 2011/2012 UK brake running change, VIN breakpoint, new caliper casting or common original part number was established.** Later US descriptions cannot prove all European details remained unchanged. Black Edition steel-disc thickness was not independently established in a European technical table in this check; avoid presenting an inferred part-level match as verified.

## Parking brake

Porsche PET, catalogue P33, model 987209, model life 2009–2012, revision 19 July 2018, illustration **603-005**, lists a handbrake shoe set, adjustment screws, springs, actuating levers and expanding bows without a base/S model restriction. Illustration **701-005** lists separate left/right parking-brake cables. This is primary parts evidence for mechanical shoe actuation separate from the hydraulic service-brake pads on the reference model, although it is a later service catalogue. It does not by itself date every part's original installation. [Original Porsche catalogue, collector copy on Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/742289471/Boxster-987-2009-2012).

Porsche workshop instruction **WM 468320**, reproduced for the 2010 Boxster S, directs removal of the rear disc to reach the parking-brake shoes and confirms cable actuation. This corroborates the shoes' location within the rear disc assembly, but the hosting page is S-labelled; use PET for the base application. [Factory service text reproduced by Operation CHARM](https://charm.li/Porsche/2010/Boxster%20S%20%28987%29%20F6-3.4L/Repair%20and%20Diagnosis/Brakes%20and%20Traction%20Control/Parking%20Brake%20System/Parking%20Brake%20Shoe/Service%20and%20Repair/).

## Wear and fluid evidence

Porsche's later technical bulletin _Brake Disc/Pad Squeal_, ATI 1605, 24 March 2016, pp.1–3, says wear depends on use; squeal can occur with certain loads, temperatures and humidity. Its inspection guidance covers glazing, friction-surface lips and corrosion. It also states a two-year brake-fluid replacement recommendation, referring to workshop instruction 470855 and the owner's maintenance booklet. This is a later general Porsche/PCNA statement, not a verified contemporary 2010 UK schedule. [Porsche-authored bulletin hosted by NHTSA](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2016/MC-10198525-9999.pdf).

A collector-hosted table titled _Maintenance intervals for Porsche vehicles_ lists brake fluid every two years for MY2010 Boxster/Boxster S, but its publication identity and market were not visually verified here. Keep it as a lead; do not use it alone as the original UK requirement. [Table](https://boxstertips.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Porsche_Maintenance_Intervals.pdf). No original, market-matched 2010 UK brake-fluid instruction was fully verified in this check.
