# Brake hardware source checks

Checked 10 November 2026. Ancillary research for chapter 13; the UK/European model-year 2010 Boxster 2.9 remains the reference. Original Porsche documents hosted by collectors are distinguished from official Porsche-hosted documents below. These are concise findings, not a complete annual parts audit.

## 2010 reference and 2009 introduction

**Porsche Cars North America, _2009 Technik Introduction: All Boxster/Cayman Models_, PNA 987 021 09, edition 2/09, printed p.119:**

| Standard iron brakes | Front, diameter × thickness | Rear, diameter × thickness | Caliper finish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boxster 2.9 / Cayman 2.9 | 318 × 28 mm | 299 × 20 mm | Black anodised |
| Boxster S / Cayman S | 318 × 28 mm | 299 × 24 mm | Red paint |

The basic models' front discs increased from 298 × 24 mm on their predecessors. Porsche explicitly describes the new base front system as the S system. The S rear discs alone are 4 mm thicker. Both use drilled, internally ventilated discs. The document identifies standard discs as grey cast iron; standard-system booster ratio becomes 5, versus 4.5 with PCCB. Yellow calipers identify factory PCCB in this document. These are design/application statements, not proof that every complete assembly interchanges. [Original Porsche training text, printed p.119](https://www.scribd.com/document/972719474/Boxster-2009).

The same document calls ABS, ABD, ASR, MSR, EBD and wheel-specific dynamic control PSM functions. Pre-filling, brake assist and Drive-Off Assistant are described as enhanced longitudinal-control additions. [Printed p.120, original Porsche text](https://www.scribd.com/document/972719474/Boxster-2009).

**Porsche, _The Boxster_, UK edition 01/10, WSLB1101000125 GB/UK:** standard Boxster and S calipers are four-piston aluminium monobloc fixed units at both axles; disc diameters are 318/299 mm. Boxster calipers are black anodised, S red. The brake booster reduces pedal effort; front openings and ducts cool the wheel brakes. The technical table names PSM with ABS 8.0. [Printed p.61 / PDF p.28](https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf#page=28) and [printed pp.126–127 / PDF p.59](https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf#page=59).

PCCB is an S option, code 450; the ordinary 2.9 has a dash in the option matrix. PCCB has 350 mm ceramic discs front/rear, six front pistons and four rear pistons, aluminium monobloc fixed calipers. Porsche describes its discs as roughly half the mass of comparable conventional discs; this is not a whole-system weight reduction. Its track-use caveat requests professional brake inspection after each event. [Printed pp.62 and 107](https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf#page=29).

**Editorial trap:** the short brochure statement about S discs being 4 mm thicker must be read with the explicit axle-by-axle training dimensions above; do not make the S front disc 32 mm thick.

## Spyder and Black Edition

**Porsche, _The Boxster Spyder_, UK edition 01/10, WSLB1101000225 GB/UK:** PCCB is optional, and the standard brakes are four-piston aluminium monobloc fixed calipers with drilled, internally ventilated discs. PSM includes ABS 8.0. [Printed p.31 / PDF p.14](https://porschecarshistory.com/wp-content/old/brochures2/93/2010%20987%20Boxster%20Spyder.pdf#page=14) and [printed p.41 / PDF p.19](https://porschecarshistory.com/wp-content/old/brochures2/93/2010%20987%20Boxster%20Spyder.pdf#page=19). Printed pp.42–43 / PDF p.20 are family-comparison prose, not the technical table.

**Porsche, _Boxster Spyder Preisliste_, Germany, 08/2010:** the standard hardware is red, four-piston fixed-caliper brakes, 318 × 28 mm front and 299 × 24 mm rear, with pad-wear monitoring. This directly verifies S-sized iron-disc dimensions on the Spyder; it does not prove identical part numbers. The UK Spyder brochure independently lists optional PCCB on printed p.31. [Printed pp.8–9 and 48–49](https://auto-preisliste.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/2010-08_preisliste_porsche_boxster-spyder.pdf).

**Porsche Cars Great Britain, _Boxster S Black Edition Price list_, edition 03/2011, LGB 200 105 85:** printed p.42 / PDF p.22 explicitly specifies 318 × 28 mm front and 299 × 24 mm rear discs, four-piston aluminium monobloc fixed calipers at both axles, vented/cross-drilled discs and ABS 8.0. The table headings explicitly say Boxster S Black Edition. Printed pp.16–17 / PDF p.9 list PCCB 450 as optional: 350 mm front/rear, six pistons front/four rear and yellow paint. The UK Black Edition brochure, printed p.13 / PDF p.7, confirms red four-piston monobloc standard calipers. [Original UK price list](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/gtavw6els2231u61qjomr/2011-987-Porsche-Boxster-Black-Edition-Price-List-UK.pdf?rlkey=7n0i52owbntdg7d17dfgyo7nw&dl=1); [original UK brochure](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/4apbqs3um5jdfryqy51ns/2011-987-Porsche-Boxster-Black-Edition-Brochure-UK.pdf?rlkey=tw7xw4lemnstkgus30glszqao&dl=1). These replace the earlier France press-release link, which now redirects to the generic Newsroom landing page.

**Porsche Cars North America, 2012 technical sheet, p.1:** all four derivatives have four-piston aluminium monobloc fixed calipers and drilled, internally ventilated standard discs. Base calipers are black; S, Black Edition and Spyder are red. It lists rounded 12.5/11.8-inch front/rear diameters for each. Use this as US corroboration; do not silently convert the rounded figures into new European dimensions. [Official 2012 sheet](https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr:f533d37f-82ef-4c9c-90cc-56b43185a8de/2012_Boxster_Specs.pdf#page=1).

The same sheet has a plainly suspect p.2 row labelled “Wheels” containing six-piston brake data and 14.15/12.99-inch discs, repeated even for the base model. Do not use that row to establish PCCB specification or availability. It conflicts with the clear contemporary UK PCCB description above.

## Annual continuity and limits

The official US 2010 technical sheet, p.2, corroborates four-piston aluminium fixed monobloc calipers, drilled/ventilated discs and 318/299 mm diameters on Boxster/S. [Official 2010 sheet](https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr:e5abc517-c680-47c0-974f-b84aefde568f/2010-Boxster-Specifications.pdf#page=2).

[Official 2010/11 sheet](https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr:48fa70bf-243a-4223-ad30-348614762824/2010-11_Boxster_Spyder_Specs.pdf#page=2).

These sources support the documented 2009 introduction and broad later hardware continuity. The factory-parts check below additionally establishes service-catalogue model-year application splits for the hydraulic unit, vacuum line and a control-system sensor. Their engineering reason, software effect, precise UK production date and VIN breakpoint remain unresolved. Later US descriptions cannot prove all European details remained unchanged. The UK Black Edition table above independently resolves its disc thicknesses. Matching dimensions still do not establish a complete-assembly interchangeability claim.

## Parking brake

Porsche PET, catalogue P33, model 987209, model life 2009–2012, revision 19 July 2018, illustration **603-005**, lists a handbrake shoe set, adjustment screws, springs, actuating levers and expanding bows without a base/S model restriction. Illustration **701-005** lists separate left/right parking-brake cables. This is primary parts evidence for mechanical shoe actuation separate from the hydraulic service-brake pads on the reference model, although it is a later service catalogue. It does not by itself date every part's original installation. [Original Porsche catalogue, PDF pp.207–208 and 225–228](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf#page=207).

Porsche workshop instruction **WM 468320**, reproduced for the 2010 Boxster S, directs removal of the rear disc to reach the parking-brake shoes and confirms cable actuation. This corroborates the shoes' location within the rear disc assembly, but the hosting page is S-labelled; use PET for the base application. [Factory service text reproduced by Operation CHARM](https://charm.li/Porsche/2010/Boxster%20S%20%28987%29%20F6-3.4L/Repair%20and%20Diagnosis/Brakes%20and%20Traction%20Control/Parking%20Brake%20System/Parking%20Brake%20Shoe/Service%20and%20Repair/).

## Wear and fluid evidence

Porsche's later technical bulletin _Brake Squeal_, ATI 1605 / 4636, 24 March 2016, pp.1–3, says wear depends on use; squeal can occur with certain loads, temperatures and humidity. Its inspection guidance covers glazing, friction-surface lips and corrosion. It also states a two-year brake-fluid replacement recommendation, referring to workshop instruction 470855 and the owner's maintenance booklet. This is a later general Porsche/PCNA statement, not a verified contemporary 2010 UK schedule. [Porsche-authored bulletin hosted by NHTSA](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2016/MC-10198525-9999.pdf).

A collector-hosted table titled _Maintenance intervals for Porsche vehicles_, dated 07/2010, lists brake fluid every two years for MY2010 Boxster/Boxster S. Its retrieved text confirms that row, but does not establish a UK-specific publication identity. Do not use it alone as the original UK requirement. [Table](https://boxstertips.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Porsche_Maintenance_Intervals.pdf). No original, market-matched 2010 UK brake-fluid instruction was fully verified in this check.

## Factory drawing check and installed neighbours — 10 November 2026

Inspected the actual Porsche PET 987209/P33, 19 July 2018, drawings and their model/PR-qualified lists. The [local factory reference](part-reference/README.md) saves 27 full-page PNGs from 12 plates with source URL, original-PDF hash, page/item/applicability records and reproduction rights. [Verification](part-reference/verification.md) enumerates all five SVGs, all three interactive IDs, every tour stage/part and every component-topic group. The factory pages remain supporting assets, rather than additional reading-column figures.

- **Front assembly:** 602-000, PDF pp.200–202. Disc 1, stationary cover 3, caliper 5, pads 6, wear lead 7, bleed valve/cap 11/12 and retaining set 15 are distinct parts. The standard front-disc numbers 996 351 405/406 01 have no model restriction on their rows; base calipers are 997 351 421/422 00, S/Spyder 996 351 425/426 11. Common dimensions/discs do not make the complete caliper assembly identical. The shared front drawing includes an external connecting line 10, but the list restricts it to **S/Spyder PR:450**: it is not assigned to the reference iron brake. The repair-set description “for rear axle” on this front plate is preserved as a catalogue oddity, not used to move its illustrated pin/spring to the rear.
- **Rear assembly:** 603-000, PDF pp.203–206. The base 987 352 401 01 rear disc differs from S/Spyder 987 352 403 01. Base calipers 986 352 423/424 01 likewise differ from S and PCCB entries. Pads, wear lead, retaining set and connecting lines are listed separately; S-specific MY2009/2010 pad/wear/damping distinctions are not made into base-model changes.
- **Rotating versus stationary neighbours:** 401-005, PDF pp.131–132, separates carrier 1, bearing 2, speed sensor 3 and hub 9. The later 991-prefixed replacement hub entries do not prove that part was originally on a 2010 car. The brake model retains the omitted carrier/bearing/speed-sensor qualification. Added the factory-backed cover, pin/spring retainers, electrical pad-wear lead and rigid-pipe/flexible-hose supply as four selectable parts, with corresponding static fallback detail. Their installed/exploded shapes, colours and paths are expressly illustrative.
- **Hydraulic supply:** 604-000/005/010/015, PDF pp.209–216, separate reservoir/servo/master, master-to-unit connections, body pipework, caliper pipes and flexible hoses. Reservoir 604-000:1 differs for manual PR:480 versus PDK PR:250; front lines differ for LHD PR:098 versus RHD PR:099. 702-000, PDF pp.229–230, also distinguishes brake pedals/pivots/caps by transmission and steering side. The chapter's common wheel-brake statement is not a claim that those assemblies are identical.
- **Vacuum route:** 104-010, PDF pp.63–64, pump 12 and line 17; 604-000:10, 604-010:22 and 604-015:14 identify the long vacuum supply and front connection. PET shows external components; training supplies the cam drive, and generic Bosch/ATE sources supply the conceptual internal booster mechanism.
- **Parts application splits:** 605-000, PDF pp.217–218, lists hydraulic unit 987 355 955 11 through MY2009 / 12 from MY2010 and sensor 997 606 145 05 through MY2010 / 07 from MY2011. It names item 5 only “Sensor”; its measured quantity is not guessed from the picture. 604-015:14 lists vacuum line 987 355 575 11 through MY2009 / 12 from MY2010. These explicit service-application boundaries improve the previous evidence gap; they do not establish a changed PSM algorithm, calibration, exact production date or reason for revision.
- **Parking brake:** 603-005, PDF pp.207–208, and 701-005, PDF pp.225/228, confirm shoe/adjuster/spring/expanding-actuator identities and independent left/right cables. Prose now names those parts. The existing Porsche workshop instruction remains the corroboration for shoe location inside the rear disc hat; an exploded shoe plate alone does not show that installed section.
