# Rear disc and parking-brake refinement

Inspected 14 November 2026. Reference: **2010 European/UK Boxster 987.2 2.9**, standard iron rear brake, not the S's 24 mm rear disc or PCCB.

## Evidence

- **Porsche PET 987209**, P33, 19 July 2018, **603-000**, PDF pp. **203–206**: rear disc **987 352 401 01** for the base model; S/Spyder **987 352 403 01** distinct. Disc, cover, fixed caliper, pads, pistons and retaining hardware are separately identified. Existing full-page renders remain in the parent directory. **603-005**, pp. **207–208**: shoe set **997 352 993 02**, adjuster item2, return springs4/5, hold-down springs6 and expanding bow11. Existing **701-005**, pp.225/228, distinguishes the left/right cables. [Porsche-authored catalogue](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf#page=203). Later service snapshot, not original production-suffix or universal-interchangeability proof.
- **Brembo Prime 09.8998.11**, [manufacturer page and technical table](https://www.bremboparts.com/europe/en/catalogue/disc/09-8998-11). The manufacturer application list includes Boxster987 2.9/188kW and OE **98735240101**. Nominal new-disc envelope: **299 mm diameter, 20 mm thickness, 67 mm overall height, 98 mm centring bore**, five mounting holes. The original photographed part shows a deep integral hat, annular drilled friction track and internal ventilation. [Original photo](https://www.bremboparts.com/en/media/product/images/1920-1920-Fix/89a4ca7f-120e-4430-b4d9-31ce9b5e21a4), unchanged as `brembo-09899811-original.jpg`, **1,500 × 1,000** pixels. Current replacement photograph; not an original 2010 supplier claim or proof of exact drilling/vane count.
- **ATE Drum Brakes catalogue 2025**, [original manufacturer PDF](https://www.ate-brakes.com/media/5615/catalogue-ate-drum-brakes-trommelbremsen-2025_v3.pdf#page=829), printed/PDF **829**, Boxster987 2.9, **155–188kW**, February2009–March2012 application with through-September2011 qualification: **03.0137-3002.2 / 653002**, **180 × 25 mm** parking shoes. Saved uncropped full-page render `ate-2025-p829.png` at1,700px long edge. This supports the reference-year nominal drum/shoe envelope, not an all-year parts claim. Full PDF SHA-256: `8da69ef0936fed588cd87adc415d27daa6e1d17cda236a8c811f1955a49f4a9d`.
- Porsche 01/10 UK brochure and 2009 Technik printed119 already cited in the chapter establish the base **299 × 20 mm** rear disc and four-piston fixed aluminium caliper. The existing workshop reference corroborates that the shoes are inside the disc assembly; PET alone is an exploded drawing rather than an installed section.

## Drawing contract

- Two independent original SVGs replace the unfolded sketch: a face view with a transparent hat, and an enlarged detail of an upper axial section. The front-facing mounting flange is transparent only to expose normally concealed shoes. Hub and caliper are omitted in that view. The section shows one opposing piston pair; the other pair lies outside the section plane.
- Face view: disc radius **149.5**, nominal drum radius **90**, centring bore radius **49**, five holes on illustrative65radius bolt circle. Outer ring versus drum proportion is **299:180**. Curved shoes and hardware follow PET, with no installed clocking, spring pitch, exact lining thickness or clearance claim.
- Section: one uniform scale of **1.7 SVG units/mm** for disc outer radius (**254.15**), thickness (**34**), overall height (**113.9**) and shoe width (**42.5**). The underlying rotation axis is y350, outer edge y95.85; mounting face x130, far disc face x243.9. The section is uniformly cropped to viewBox102.5/70/205/210, so the rotation axis is below the visible detail. Main envelope is not stretched independently to make the hat look longer. The caliper/pistons, shoe web, plate split, hat-wall thickness, fillets and air gap remain explanatory.
- No label leaders or movement arrows. The shared React comparison component places numbered markers directly over their parts and a native text key below. Percentages in `markers.json` refer to each displayed SVG viewBox, including the face view's -8px assembly translation and the cropped section detail. Original images contain no baked editorial numbers.
