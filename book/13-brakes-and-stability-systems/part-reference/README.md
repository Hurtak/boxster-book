# Factory parts references: brakes and stability systems

Reference car: **2010 European/UK Boxster 987.2 2.9, six-speed manual**, with PDK identified as an alternative. Checked 10 November 2026 against the actual drawings and their associated lists, rather than search-result thumbnails.

## Source and reproduction

Porsche, *PET Boxster 2009–2012*, model **987209**, catalogue **P33**, snapshot **19 July 2018**, 583 PDF pages. [Porsche-authored catalogue, independently hosted](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf).

Original PDF SHA-256: `c865de9263f5946db4c24874eb10e503be2343762b308695aa7bb6a943bfd2bf`.

The PNGs are full, unaltered PDF pages rasterised with Poppler at a maximum dimension of 1,800 px. Page numbers below are **one-based PDF page numbers**, not the plate's internal `-1`, `-2` numbering. The drawings' Porsche identity, item numbers, catalogue/date and model-life headers remain visible. Original copyright remains Porsche's; these factory excerpts are supporting references, **not MIT-licensed original book artwork**. The full PDF is not duplicated here.

PET's own option-code index identifies **098 LHD / 099 RHD** (PDF p.8), **250 PDK** (p.9), **450 PCCB / 480 six-speed manual** (p.11). These codes qualify catalogue rows; they do not prove the 2010 UK retail option catalogue. In particular, PR:450 rows must not turn PCCB into an ordinary 2.9 option. Contemporary January 2010 UK brochure evidence in [research.md](../research.md) establishes that restriction.

## What this evidence can establish

- Separate installed assemblies and their connections: disc/pads/caliper versus cover and retainers; electrical wear lead versus hydraulic hose; hub/bearing/carrier versus rotating disc; servo/master cylinder versus ABS hydraulic unit; mechanical parking shoes and cables versus service pads.
- Explicit parts-list applications, including base/S/Spyder, PCCB, manual/PDK, LHD/RHD and the printed model-year splits. The catalogue does not give engine codes on the brake rows. Its unrestricted rows are not permission to apply every optional assembly to every model.
- Later replacement-part relationships, when marked. A 2018 service entry or a 991/981-prefixed replacement does not establish what was fitted in 2010, that a complete assembly interchanges, or why an engineering revision occurred.

## Limits and independent principle sources

PET does **not** establish exact installed coordinates, material/nominal disc dimensions, pad compounds, piston travel, internal fluid routing, hydraulic circuit allocation, pressure calibration, vacuum level, sensor calibration, intervention timing or safe workshop procedures. The 3D model remains an explanatory approximation. The front PET figure's shared variant drawing cannot override PR/model qualifications in the adjacent list.

The existing Porsche January 2010 UK brochure and 2009 technical introduction own brake materials/dimensions, piston counts and control functions. The MY2010 handbook owns the axle-split circuit statement and driver-facing conditions. Porsche training owns the bank 4–6 exhaust-cam vacuum-pump drive; PET identifies the external pump and lines, not its internals. Bosch/ATE explain generic booster principles; pressure/force and kinetic-energy figures are educational physics. The archived Porsche parking-brake workshop instruction corroborates shoes inside the rear disc assembly; the PET gives the base-model parts application.
