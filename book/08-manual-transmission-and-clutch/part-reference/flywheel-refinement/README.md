# Dual-mass flywheel references — 14 November 2026

Reference: 2010 European/UK Boxster 987.2 2.9, six-speed manual. The part photos guide the visible face; the manufacturer guide explains a generic internal mechanism. Those are different evidence scopes.

## Part identity and photographs

[Wolf Auto Parts, PS85488](https://wolfautoparts.com/parts/dual-mass-flywheel-clutch-pressure-plate-9g211401202-ps85488/) identifies its used flywheel/clutch/pressure-plate set as **9G2 114 012 02**. The complete [PET 102-000 item list, PDF p. 41](../pet-102-000-p041.png) lists that flywheel for **A120 + PR480**, alongside earlier 00/01 entries. The A121 manual uses a separate 20/21/22 family, and PR250 PDK has separate 020-series entries. The existing [factory drawing, PDF p. 39](../pet-102-000-p039.png), identifies flywheel 14 and the ten crankshaft bolts 15. A service suffix is not an original-production date or proof of complete clutch interchangeability.

The supplier's fourth photograph is a separated set: flywheel at lower left, driven plate at lower right, cover above. The first shows the clutch installed on the flywheel; its diaphragm fingers belong to the clutch cover, not the flywheel's internal damper. The second is a cover detail. Original JPEGs are retained unchanged. The manuscript uses views 4 and 1 as separate React panels; view 4 has a source-coordinate viewport crop `[90,190,365,343]`, without exporting a modified bitmap.

## Manufacturer mechanism reference

Schaeffler Automotive Aftermarket GmbH & Co. KG, **LuK Dual Mass Flywheel: Technology / Failure Diagnosis / Testing Tool / User Instructions**, **October 2019** colophon, 52 pages. The saved PDF has creation metadata dated May 2023; that is not treated as the copyright/edition date. [Manufacturer-hosted reference](https://api-aftermarket.schaeffler.de/medias/BR-0011-en.PDF?context=bWFzdGVyfGRvY3VtZW50c3w0ODIxNzc4fGFwcGxpY2F0aW9uL3BkZnxkb2N1bWVudHMvaDY4L2gxYS85Mzc4NjE0NTc1MTM0LnBkZnw2ZjVlNTdmMWM4MWU2ZTUzOTQxZDI5NGJkMGI3MWZhMGYzYmIyMmQzZjg5Y2ViOGYwNDA4MjA2NzNkMmQ1MzQ4); [downloaded manufacturer-authored copy, independent host](https://www.piecesautos.tn/static/piece_docs/Schaeffler_LuK_BR_0011_en.PDF).

- **PDF/printed p. 9:** primary housing, starter ring, arc-spring channels, secondary mass and output flange.
- **p. 12:** secondary mass's clutch-contact face, mounting surface and openings.
- **p. 15:** flange wings engage the arc springs; shows several alternative flange designs.
- **p. 18:** curved coil-spring arrangements, including different nested/staged alternatives.

All four retained full-page renderings were visually inspected. The official copy was read with the web PDF viewer; direct downloads from the manufacturer returned 403, so the identified independent copy supplied the local renderings. The guide is generic, later technical evidence: it does not identify this Porsche's hidden spring count, nested stages, exact flange, bearing type or allowable twist. No centrifugal pendulum or other modern variant is assigned to the reference car.

## Drawing decisions

The old pair of washer-like circles is replaced with one clutch-side exterior and one explicitly labelled teaching cutaway, each showing the masses as coaxial parts of a compact assembly. Photo-guided ratios give the friction annulus more area and reduce the mounting centre. The face gains ten mounting locations, inner openings, machining rings, starter teeth and cover attachments. Only the central bolt count is supported as an exact fitment detail; tooth detail and other small features are illustrative.

In the generic cutaway, coils follow curved channels and meet fixed primary stops and the secondary flange's wing faces. The dashed flange is a reference position, not another disc; the solid flange is slightly turned. Its pointer indexes refer to those two positions. The pictured twist, spring turns, hidden clearances and dimensions are not Porsche service limits. Numbered leaders terminate on the corresponding drawn components; the key is native React/HTML, separate from the two SVG panels. The comparison stacks them in narrow viewports.
