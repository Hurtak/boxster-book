## What these references establish

The original drawings establish component identities, the separate slave/lever/bearing/guide arrangement, paired cable assembly, casing interfaces and hydraulic branches. They do not establish exact installation coordinates, factory gear tooth counts, synchroniser cone counts, force/travel, DMF internal design or workshop removal order. No internal manual gearset plate is supplied in this catalogue; the two illustrated pairs in the book remain a generic principle model.

The source's 2018 replacement state does not prove 2010 UK option orderability, an original part's uninterrupted use, or interchangeability of complete assemblies. Period Porsche training, catalogue and handbook sources in `../research.md` remain the authorities for manual ratios, 240 mm clutch, S X-Tend, driver operation and option scope.
