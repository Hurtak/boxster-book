## Coordinating driving systems

- **Demand and DME:** Bosch [accelerator-pedal module, operating principle](https://www.bosch-mobility.com/en/solutions/air-management/accelerator-pedal-module/) supports generic pedal demand → requested engine torque. Porsche *2009 Technik Introduction*, printed Fuel/ignition **58**, [viewer **64**](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3550487/Porsche-Boxster-2009.html?page=64), identifies the **2.9 ME 7.8.2** controller and PDK communication. Main text links to chapter06 rather than repeating sensor and combustion tutorials.
- **PDK/DME cooperation:** same technical introduction, printed Transmission **72–74**, viewer **78–80**, covers mode-dependent shift torque treatment. [Viewer 80](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3550487/Porsche-Boxster-2009.html?page=80) was directly inspected: SPORT PLUS does **not** use the same torque-reduction treatment as normal mode. Therefore the interactive says “engine torque treatment”, not a universal torque cut. UK 01/10, printed **42–45**, establishes optional PDK and overlap shift. The broad electronic-control interaction is illustrated, without inventing topology, packet routing or millisecond timing.
- **PSM:** MY2010 owner manual printed **63–65**, [PDF 65–67](https://billswebspace.com/Owners_Manual_Boxster_PCNA.pdf#page=65), lists speed, steering angle, lateral acceleration, yaw and longitudinal acceleration, engine-output reduction and individual-wheel braking. Porsche *2009 Technik Introduction*, printed Chassis **120–121**, [viewer **126–127**](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3550487/Porsche-Boxster-2009.html?page=126), establishes standard PSM, prefill, brake assist and drive-off support. The interaction's urgent-stop example selects the circumstances for prefill/assist/ABS; it does not say all are always active.
- **PASM and Sport Chrono:** UK 01/10 printed **54–57**, PDF **25–26**; printed **107**, PDF **49**, codes **475, 639, 640**. PASM changes controlled wheel damping and has Normal/Sport ranges; Chrono modifies response/PSM/PDK strategy and selects PASM Sport if PASM is fitted. Plus requires PCM. PDK/PASM not bundled by implication. Printed **126–127** retains **188 kW / 255 PS** for the 2.9.

## Original diagrams and interaction contract

Three original explanatory diagrams:

1. **`belt-functions.svg`**, static chapter-root original: belt load path, reel locking, pretensioner and force limiter. No timing, force, travel or internal calibration.
2. **`fixed-rollover-structure.svg`**, static chapter-root original: permanent front/rear structure and upper belt anchors. No geometry or predicted survivable clearance.
