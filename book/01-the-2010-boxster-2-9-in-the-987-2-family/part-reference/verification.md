## Checks and remaining limits

All sixteen saved factory pages were viewed, and the two photo source spreads and luggage/roof spreads were inspected. The selector module passes `node --check`; all three original SVGs parse as XML. A local check verifies the unchanged heading list, diagram ID and **12** footnote references in their original order. The two photos match the source PDF's extracted JPEG bytes. Main book builds, browser rendering and responsive interaction checks are reserved for parent integration; no global build, shared edit, staging or nested agent was used.

No new factual contradiction was found in the original figures. The unresolved limits are the existing ones: exact first retail delivery/order dates in every market, complete annual UK equipment continuity, hidden photograph equipment, original-fitment interchangeability and precise installation geometry are not established and are not asserted. The family selector has four meaningful model choices; the 5–10 component-group guideline for 3D explorers does not apply.
