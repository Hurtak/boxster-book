## Evidence boundaries

The catalogue is a **2018 service-parts snapshot**. Replacements, supersessions, shared illustration art and generic equipment-code descriptions are not proof of identical original production parts, retail standard equipment, calibration or complete interchangeability. Broad mounting-time ranges on p.1 put both later derivatives at 08/10; the dated 2009 Spyder announcement and 2011 Black Edition announcement remain the chronology authorities.

Drawings support assembly identity and visible connections. The brochure/technical sheet establishes longitudinal mid-engine packaging, flat-six construction, rear-wheel drive, two-seat body, ordinary powered hood, luggage usability, output and transmission choices. PET does not establish exact installation coordinates, material, crash loads, yaw inertia, motion timing, control behaviour or performance.
