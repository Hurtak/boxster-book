# Chapter 01 research: locating the reference car

Checked 10 November 2026. Scope is the family foundation, packaging and reading conventions; the annual chronology and option catalogue belong to chapters 02 and 03. This note records primary Porsche material independently inspected for this draft. A collector's hosting does not make an original Porsche publication a secondary source. UK equipment claims use UK material.

## Contemporary UK reference brochure

Porsche, *The Boxster*, UK edition **01/10**, colophon **WSLB1101000125**, ©2010. Exact source:

https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf

Web tool opened the PDF; local `/tmp/boxster-ch05/brochure-2010.pdf` and its text supplied extraction and image inspection. The source contains 62 PDF pages comprising multi-page spreads, so PDF and printed numbering differ.

- Printed **8–10**, PDF **4**: ordinary Boxster 2.9, 188 kW/255 DIN hp and 290 Nm; S 3.4, 228 kW/310 DIN hp and 360 Nm. Both six-speed manual; seven-speed PDK optional. Photograph separately labels the ordinary green car and S. Used for reference specification and the front photograph.
- Printed **20–21**, PDF **8**: ordinary “The Boxster” model section. Rear photograph used as the second real image. The images are individually embedded photographic objects, not screenshots of unreadable brochure pages.
- Printed **78–79**, PDF **37**: two luggage compartments, 150 litres front and 130 rear, including with the hood lowered. Rear luggage compartment has service hatch for engine-oil/coolant filling. Supports packaging prose; not a claim of full engine access.
- Printed **80–81**, PDF **38**: ordinary powered hood description, heated glass screen. Only the broad powered-roof concept enters the selector.
- Printed **126–127**, PDF **59**: engine capacities 2,893/3,436 cm³; peak outputs and torque; rear-wheel drive; six-speed manual and optional seven-speed PDK; luggage volume. UK length is 4,342 mm, unlike the PCNA sheet below. Length is deliberately unnecessary to this introductory chapter.
- Colophon, PDF **62**: publication identity, independent of host filename “2010-2011”.

The manuscript's PS convention retains the European DIN numerical figures printed as “hp” rather than calling them bhp. No new conversion is calculated. The editorial reference is model-year 2010 European/UK; the brochure's January publication does not establish the build date or exact options of any individual car.

## Porsche 987 generation grouping

Porsche Classic, *Model Boxster (type 987)*:

https://www.porsche.com/netherlands/nl/service/classic/models/987b/987b/

Opened in browser tool. The page groups ordinary cars as **2005–2008** and **2009–2012**, with 2.9-litre engine in the latter. It identifies 987 as the type and 2005 as its first model year. The chapter explicitly describes decimal suffixes as the book's practical phase shorthand, rather than suggesting they were printed catalogue model names. Classic's “Spyder (2011)” grouping is not substituted for contemporary February 2010 European market arrival.

## Contemporary facelift release and Cayman distinction

Porsche Netherlands, **19 November 2008**, *De nieuwe Porsche Boxster en Cayman: krachtiger en zuiniger*:

https://nieuws.porsche.nl/de-nieuwe-porsche-boxster-en-cayman-krachtiger-en-zuiniger/

Opened and read. Opening paragraphs give Boxster **2.9, 255 pk/188 kW**, Cayman **2.9, 265 pk/195 kW** and S outputs. PDK section makes six-speed manual standard for all ordinary models and says PDK replaces Tiptronic S. Chassis section describes revised tuning/steering and enhanced PSM; styling section front/rear revisions and LED rear lights. This supports the major phase distinction and cautions against treating the fixed-roof Cayman as mechanically identical in every application. No Dutch equipment package is treated as UK standard equipment.

## Layout and injection: contemporary technical sheet

Porsche Cars North America, *2010 Boxster specifications*, 3 pages:

https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr:e5abc517-c680-47c0-974f-b84aefde568f/2010-Boxster-Specifications.pdf

Opened in browser tool.

- **PDF p.1**: horizontally opposed, mid-mounted six-cylinder; engine/transmission common drive unit, rear-wheel drive. Sequential multipoint injection on 2.9, DFI on S. Supports mechanical layout and injection distinction without US/UK trim inference.
- **PDF p.2**: two-seat roadster body.
- Not adopted: 2.9 bore entry 88 mm conflicts with displacement and Porsche's technical-training material used in chapter05. Sheet also prints a suspect S sixth ratio (0.081) and a 4,372 mm length unlike UK 4,342 mm. Neither bore, ratios nor length are needed in this chapter. A source is used for its clear corroborated claims, not presumed infallible as a whole.

The schematic places the engine before the rear axle, behind the seats, with the gearbox behind the engine. Front/rear luggage location is supported by UK brochure illustrations and text. Positions, sizes and component boundaries in the SVG are illustrative, not measured CAD. The yaw paragraph is a qualitative mechanics explanation rather than a measured claim about Porsche's mass distribution or stability limit.

https://nieuws.porsche.nl/porsche-boxster-spyder-lichtgewicht-roadster-voor-puristen/

## Successor boundary

Porsche Netherlands, **12 January 2012**, *Porsche presenteert nieuwe Boxster*:

https://nieuws.porsche.nl/porsche-presenteert-nieuwe-boxster/

Opened and read. New-generation chassis, longer wheelbase, greater track width and newly developed electromechanical steering. Engine section identifies ordinary **2.7-litre, 195 kW/265 pk**, both ordinary engines direct-injected. Dutch dealer arrival **14 April 2012**. Used to distinguish the successor, not to establish a universal final 987 build date or numerical model-year cut-off.

## Evidence limits

No annual sequence of original UK price lists was reconstructed here. No claim that all 2009–2012 equipment remained unchanged is made. Photographs are captioned by variant and publication, not used to identify hidden options. Service-catalogue cautions are editorial reading guidance; no PET listing is used as original-production evidence. Chapter01 does not attempt individual VIN decoding, engine-code identification, or a buying/servicing checklist.
