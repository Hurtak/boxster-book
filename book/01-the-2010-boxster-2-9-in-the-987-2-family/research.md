## Contemporary UK reference brochure

Porsche, *The Boxster*, UK edition **01/10**, colophon **WSLB1101000125**, ©2010. Exact source:

https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf

Web tool opened the PDF; local `/tmp/boxster-ch05/brochure-2010.pdf` and its text supplied extraction and image inspection. The source contains 62 PDF pages comprising multi-page spreads, so PDF and printed numbering differ.

- Printed **8–10**, PDF **4**: ordinary Boxster 2.9, 188 kW/255 DIN hp and 290 Nm; S 3.4, 228 kW/310 DIN hp and 360 Nm. Both six-speed manual; seven-speed PDK optional. Photograph separately labels the ordinary green car and S. Used for reference specification and the front photograph.
- Printed **20–21**, PDF **8**: ordinary “The Boxster” model section. Rear photograph used as the second real image. The images are individually embedded photographic objects, not screenshots of unreadable brochure pages.
- Printed **78–79**, PDF **37**: two luggage compartments, 150 litres front and 130 rear, including with the hood lowered. Rear luggage compartment has service hatch for engine-oil/coolant filling. Supports packaging prose; not a claim of full engine access.
- Printed **80–81**, PDF **38**: ordinary powered hood description, heated glass screen. Only the broad powered-roof concept enters the selector.
- Printed **126–127**, PDF **59**: engine capacities 2,893/3,436 cm³; peak outputs and torque; rear-wheel drive; six-speed manual and optional seven-speed PDK; luggage volume. UK length is 4,342 mm, unlike the PCNA sheet below. Length is deliberately unnecessary to this introductory chapter.
- Colophon, PDF **61**: publication identity, independent of host filename “2010-2011”.

The manuscript's PS convention retains the European DIN numerical figures printed as “hp” rather than calling them bhp. No new conversion is calculated. The editorial reference is model-year 2010 European/UK; the brochure's January publication does not establish the build date or exact options of any individual car.
