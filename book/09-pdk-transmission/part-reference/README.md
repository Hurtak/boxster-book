## What these sources establish

The saved PET pages establish the external assembly, catalogue applications, bolted sump, separate hydraulic/electronic units, flanges, mechanical cable, support brackets and exchanger plumbing. The 2018 parts snapshot does **not** establish original option availability, all production changes, installation coordinates, forces, materials, service procedure or calibration. The generic PR220 key wording is not used to infer this car's differential locking percentages.

Internal operating evidence remains Porsche AfterSales Training, *2009 Technik Introduction — All Boxster/Cayman Models*, **PNA 987 021 09, edition 2/09**, [Porsche-authored full text at Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/972719474/Boxster-2009) and [page-indexed mirror](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3550487/Porsche-Boxster-2009.html): printed **68, 80–92** for shafts, gear selection, flywheel, clutch packs and parking catch; **93–102** for hydraulics, cooling, sensing and separate ECU. Freshly read on 10 November 2026. Individual ManualsLib page 94 could not be fetched; the complete archive supplied the training text. PET alone cannot confirm the nested clutch/shaft topology or an overlap shift.

The period UK brochure, owner's manual and variant sources remain documented in [research.md](../research.md). They establish UK optional fitment, driver controls, ratios and performance qualifications. See [verification.md](verification.md) for the complete topic/figure/model disposition.
