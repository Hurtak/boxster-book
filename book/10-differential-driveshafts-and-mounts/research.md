## Original diagrams and calculation contract

- **`drivetrain-open-differential`**: one D3 figure with native **Speed averaging** / **Unequal grip** buttons. Initial `view=speed`, carrier **300 rpm**, difference **20%**, left **240 rpm**, right **360 rpm**; poster matches. Speed model: left = carrier × (1 − percentage/100); right = carrier × (1 + percentage/100). At difference 0: **300/300**; at 100: **0/600**. Carrier control **50–600 rpm**, step 10; difference **0–100%**, step 5. Speeds are prescribed kinematics, not a slip solver.
- Grip view initial left capacity **100 Nm**, fixed right capacity **600 Nm**, requested axle torque **800 Nm**: each = min(request/2, left capacity, right capacity) = **100 Nm**, total **200 Nm**, unsustainable remainder **600 Nm**. Left control **0–600 Nm**, step 25; demand **0–1,200 Nm**, step 50. At left=600, request=800 → **400 each, 800 total**; at left=600, request=1,200 → **600 each, 1,200 total**; left=0 → **0 each/total**. Explicit ideal steady unbraked open differential, no losses, inertia, tyre map, LSD map or PSM intervention.
- Stable host dataset contract: `drivetrainView`, `drivetrainLeftRpm`, `drivetrainRightRpm`, `drivetrainCarrierRpm`, `drivetrainEachNm`, `drivetrainTotalNm`. Values persist in runtime state across unmount/remount. Buttons and ranges bind to supplied abort signal; shared SVG helper owns resize-observer disconnection. No timers or automatic movement.
- **`images/drivetrain-layout.svg`**: static original plan-view bridge showing engine, transaxle, final drive, two half-shafts and schematic three support locations. It is not measured mount spacing or workshop lifting guidance.

## Independent full text and source audit — 10 November 2026

Material corrections and source improvements:

- The open-differential caption previously called 240/360 rpm at a 300 rpm carrier a “20 percent speed difference”. These are **−20%/+20% offsets from carrier speed**; the full output-speed difference is 120 rpm, or 40% of carrier speed. Caption now names the offsets. The model and slider were already correct and are retained.
- Corrected the Spyder evidence locator from **PDF p. 8 to PDF p. 13**, retaining printed **pp. 28–29**. The actual spread was rendered and viewed; the standard-differential statement is explicit. The UK identity/edition was independently checked at PDF p. **23**: **01/10, WSLB1101000225 GB/UK**.

E.
