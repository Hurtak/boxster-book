# Diagram review

Reviewed 10 November 2026. Reference: the 2010 European/UK Boxster 987.2 2.9-litre.

This records the figure-review pass. Subsequent source-driven wording and geometry corrections are recorded in the [text and source review](text-and-source-review.md).

The original **59 logical diagrams** received individual reviews. A further **14 component tours** now extend the brake-style 3D breakdown to every parts chapter. The book contains **73 logical diagrams**: **35 standalone SVGs** and **38 interactive figures**, each with a static fallback. One dedicated sub-agent handled each original diagram and each new component tour; the book-wide checks below cover their integration.

## Material corrections

- Corrected the four-stroke cycle at 720°, damper telescoping and force conventions, clutch-disc/release geometry, wheel-bolt holes and pad fit.
- Distinguished two catalyst bricks within each manifold/converter assembly; aligned the exhaust and sensor prose with the revised figure. Clarified road force on the tyres during forward braking.
- Made phase comparisons use equal lift, replaced auto-rescaling with fixed energy and speed scales so input changes remain visible, and corrected the false equation caused by independently rounding half-rpm differential outputs.
- Added component connections, motion/force/flow directions and readable labels: roof frame/glass/cover, rotary drive shafts, water collection/drainage, steering assistance, suspension location, electrical loops and audio paths.
- Clarified equipment dependencies and the boundaries of the examples. Corrected the unsupported inference that a digital BOSE amplifier establishes the CDR-30 feed type.

## Validation

- `npm test`: **31 tests passed**, including portable, web and GitHub Pages builds.
- All 73 source SVGs parse as XML, have accessible titles/descriptions and keep their text within a 342 px canvas.
- Every interactive figure mounts and responds to native controls at **390, 768 and 1440 px**; checks cover slider ends, buttons, selectors, live label bounds and horizontal overflow.
- Individual reviews also check significant mechanical/numerical states and matching fallbacks at phone and desktop widths. Additional regression checks cover cycle/valve boundaries, differential arithmetic, fixed chart references, body-force signs, roof-state relationships and printed fallback size.
- Offline assets, models, cross-chapter navigation, keyboard use, source-note disclosures and static/print fallbacks remain covered by the book suite. Tall printed figures are constrained to 220 mm.
- Independent renders of all 59 SVGs found no text clipping or overlapping labels. The revised artwork and representative live phone, tablet and desktop views were visually inspected.
- Suspended interactive views preserve their measured space even when the new poster is taller. Returning to a hidden chapter restores its controls, and printing still shows the complete fallback.

## Scope of the drawings

These are explanatory schematics. Shapes, intermediate positions, fluid routing, timing and synthetic parameters remain illustrative unless a specific source value is identified. The figures do not supply workshop dimensions, operating procedures, complete factory configurators, measured rolling circumferences or crash/deployment predictions. The individual captions and chapter research identify the relevant limits.

All 59 original logical diagrams were revised, and 14 component tours with matching fallbacks were added. Existing photographs and their credits were preserved.
