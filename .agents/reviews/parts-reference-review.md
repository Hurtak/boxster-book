# Factory parts reference review

Review baseline: commit `90b95859b9c140cac30d827c36ab45a632792f1e`. Reference car: model-year 2010 European/UK Boxster 987.2 2.9, six-speed manual, with PDK and other equipment identified separately.

## Source and method

The chapter folders contain selected, unaltered page images from Porsche's **PET catalogue 987209, model life 2009–2012, P33, dated 19 July 2018**. The Porsche-authored PDF is [hosted by an independent site](https://www.porsche-preparation.com/downloaded/pet-porsche/boxster-2009-2012-type-987.pdf). It was downloaded on 10 November 2026: 583 pages, 7,398,378 bytes, SHA-256 `c865de9263f5946db4c24874eb10e503be2343762b308695aa7bb6a943bfd2bf`. Chapter `part-reference/README.md` files record the original one-based PDF page numbers, illustration numbers and relevant applicability; their verification records map these to the existing prose, static illustrations and interactive figures.

The atlas and ordering checks also archive selected full pages from two Porsche-authored period publications, both freshly downloaded and checksum-matched to the research copies:

- *The Boxster*, UK edition 01/10, WSLB1101000125 GB/UK: [source PDF](https://mrsportscars.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Porsche-Boxster-987-Gen-2-Sales-Brochure-UK-US-2010-2011.pdf), SHA-256 `69b7491a26a7ec3609a0ef92299f276f158b311207e8cad1596c8049ac4031ac`.
- *Boxster S Black Edition*, UK price list 03/2011: [source PDF](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/gtavw6els2231u61qjomr/2011-987-Porsche-Boxster-Black-Edition-Price-List-UK.pdf?rlkey=7n0i52owbntdg7d17dfgyo7nw&dl=0), SHA-256 `7bad256748bf6c762eca7543be1ee34b43409748e610b89ec93c19e5d0792d23`.

The [official Porsche UK Classic catalogue page](https://www.porsche.com/uk/accessoriesandservice/classic/originalpartscatalogue/), checked on the same date, lists a 986 catalogue but no 987 download. The mirror is identified explicitly rather than presented as an official Porsche download address.

Drawings and adjacent parts/application lists are read together. The reference 2.9 application is distinguished from the S and special models, and manual/PDK, steering position and equipment restrictions are retained where relevant. The original drawings remain Porsche material; the repository's MIT licence does not relicense them.

PET supports component identification and service-assembly relationships. A 2018 service-parts listing alone does not establish original 2010 retail availability, the introduction date of a replacement, VIN-specific interchangeability, exact installed geometry, material, control calibration, fluid flow or mechanism timing. Operating explanations retain the relevant period brochure, handbook and technical-training evidence. The chapter-owned 3D assemblies remain educational schematics; their exploded offsets are not workshop removal instructions or measured operating travel.

## Evidence traps retained in the record

- PET's generic PR220 key says “40%”; the model-specific Porsche training/brochure figures remain 22% drive and 27% overrun.
- Later catalogue “mounting time” rows are not substitutes for contemporary Spyder/Black Edition launch announcements. A later 235 kW/319 Hp entry does not silently replace the dated European 320 PS specification.
- A shared front-brake drawing includes a connecting pipe qualified for PR450. It is not assigned to the standard 2.9 brake.
- The PDK support plate reuses a manual-looking case outline. Application lists support the mount identities, not literal PDK case geometry.
- PR098/099 steering position is distinct from traffic-side beam codes; generic codes do not establish market orderability.
- The UK Black Edition 03/2011 price list explicitly lists manual-only XCZ. This resolves that derivative's listing, while ordinary 2010 UK factory eligibility remains unverified. The XLF/XLS exclusion alone does not establish separate XLS retail availability.
- Wheel spoke designs, colours and upholstery images are checked against the dated original brochure artwork. A generic service drawing is not used to identify a photographed wheel design.
