# Porsche Boxster 987.2: Europe/UK chronology

Research checked 10 November 2026. This is an outline reference, not a claim to have reconstructed every UK price-list revision. Links below are Porsche-authored contemporary material, including original brochures hosted by collectors; US material is explicitly identified.

The book's editorial baseline is the **model-year 2010 Boxster 2.9-litre**. Use the January 2010 UK ordinary-model brochure as a starting source for that reference car, with dated order guides to establish exact equipment. The chronology and other variants below supply comparisons; they do not change the baseline. Keep manual and optional PDK configurations explicit, since no particular owner's gearbox or option list has been specified.

## Dating rules

- Keep **announcement, calendar-year availability, model year and first registration separate**. The facelift announcement is dated 19 November 2008; Porsche SE's April 2009 release confirms availability since February 2009. It should not become a “2008-model-year 987.2” merely because it was announced in 2008. [Porsche launch release][launch]; [Porsche SE contemporary confirmation, p.2][arrival]
- The ordinary facelift is grouped as **2009–2012** by Porsche Classic. Its page labels the Spyder “2011”, whereas Porsche's contemporary European release says it would reach the market in **February 2010**. Record both labels with their context instead of turning the announced February 2010 introduction into a 2011 calendar event. [Porsche Classic][classic]; [Porsche Netherlands, 6 November 2009][spyder-news]
- Porsche’s Dutch announcement of 2 February 2011 specified Black Edition availability from **March 2011**; that prospective statement is not a record of completed deliveries across Europe. Porsche Cars North America's release calls it a **2012** model. The UK price list is Edition **03/2011** and says its prices apply to vehicles in the model year beginning **1 March 2011**, without assigning a numerical model year there. Do not import the US “2012” label as the European introduction date. [European release][black-news]; [PCNA release][black-us]; [UK price list, cover and p.5][black-prices]

[Launch][launch]; [Availability confirmation][arrival]; [Porsche SE, 2 October 2008][design2] |

[Porsche Netherlands, 19 November 2008][facelift-news] |
| **2010** | The January 2010 UK brochure retains the ordinary Boxster/S capacities, outputs and transmissions. The substantive range addition is the Spyder, announced in November 2009 for market introduction in February 2010. | **2011** | The Black Edition was announced in February, with availability specified from March. Its release still compares its 320 PS with the ordinary S's 310 PS. This supports output continuity, not a claim that every ordinary-model trim, price or option was unchanged. [Black Edition release][black-news] |
| **2012** | Treat the 987.2 as the outgoing range, without inventing a further power increase or facelift. Porsche's US 2012 specification sheet still lists the same four outputs and manual/PDK choices; this corroborates the mechanical picture but is not UK equipment evidence. The new-generation 981 was announced in January, with Dutch dealer availability specified from 14 April 2012. [2012 specifications][specs-2012]; [Porsche Netherlands, 12 January 2012][981-news] |

## Predecessor and comparison specifications

Immediately before the facelift, the ordinary cars were the **2,687 cc Boxster, 180 kW/245 DIN hp**, and **3,387 cc S, 217 kW/295 DIN hp**. The base manual had five speeds, the S six; optional Tiptronic S had five. [Original preceding-generation brochure, pp.110–111][old-brochure]

| 987.2 variant | Engine and published European output | Transmissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Boxster** | 2,893 cc; 188 kW/255 DIN hp at 6,400 rpm; 290 Nm at 4,400–6,000 rpm. [UK launch brochure, pp.126–127][standard-2009] | Six-speed manual; optional seven-speed PDK. [Same source][standard-2009] |
| **Boxster S** | 3,436 cc; DFI; 228 kW/310 DIN hp at 6,400 rpm; 360 Nm at 4,400–5,500 rpm. [UK brochure, pp.8–10 and 126–127][standard-2010] | Six-speed manual; optional seven-speed PDK. [Same source][standard-2010] |
| **Boxster Spyder** | 3,436 cc; DFI; 235 kW/320 DIN hp at 7,200 rpm; 370 Nm at 4,750 rpm. [UK Spyder brochure, pp.24–27 and 41][spyder-brochure] | Six-speed manual; optional seven-speed PDK. [Same source][spyder-brochure] |
| **Boxster S Black Edition** | 3.4 litres; DFI; 235 kW/320 DIN hp at 7,200 rpm; 370 Nm at 4,750 rpm. [European release][black-news]; [UK brochure, pp.10–13][black-brochure] | Six-speed manual; optional seven-speed PDK. [UK brochure, pp.12–13][black-brochure] |

The 2.9-litre base engine uses multipoint injection; the S, Spyder and Black Edition use DFI. The official 2012 sheet makes that distinction explicit. Avoid “all 987.2s are direct-injected.” [Porsche 2012 specifications, p.1][specs-2012]

**Power-unit editorial rule:** reproduce the kW/DIN pairings above. The English brochures label their DIN figures “hp”; Dutch Porsche releases use “pk”. Do not silently relabel 320 as 320 bhp/SAE hp. [UK technical tables][standard-2009]; [Spyder technical table][spyder-brochure]

## What defines the two later variants

**Spyder:** a distinct production variant alongside Boxster and S, rather than a numbered limited edition. Porsche's contemporary release explicitly distinguishes it from earlier limited-series Boxsters carrying the Spyder name. [Spyder release][spyder-news]

Its UK brochure specifies a two-piece manually assembled lightweight hood, aluminium doors and rear deck, lighter interior with fabric door pulls, standard sports bucket seats, 19-inch Spyder wheels, a fixed spoiler, sports suspension 20 mm lower than the S, and a standard mechanical locking differential. DIN weight is **1,275 kg manual / 1,300 kg PDK**. Radio and air conditioning are omitted from the basic lightweight specification but offered as options. Sport Chrono and sports exhaust are optional. The technical table distinguishes hood-open maximum speeds (**267/265 km/h**, manual/PDK) from the **200 km/h hood-closed limit**. [UK Spyder brochure, pp.12–17, 28–35, 38–41][spyder-brochure]

**Black Edition:** limited to **987 cars**, with black exterior, hood, intake detailing, exhaust outlets, rollover bars and 19-inch Spyder wheels; black interior detailing, stainless sill plates and a numbered glovebox plaque. It retains the ordinary electrically operated hood. [UK brochure, pp.6–7 and 16–27][black-brochure]; [UK price list, pp.10–13][black-prices]

The UK standard equipment includes SportDesign steering wheel (paddles with PDK), partial leather seats, automatic climate control, cruise, dimming mirrors/rain sensor, wind deflector, PCM/navigation, universal audio interface, phone preparation, Sound Package Plus, and bi-xenon lights with dynamic cornering and LED DRLs. **PDK, PASM, mechanical locking differential, PCCB, Sport Chrono Plus and sports exhaust remain options.** UK recommended retail price was **£47,426** under the March 2011 list. [UK price list, pp.6 and 8–17][black-prices]

## Evidence limits and useful follow-up

- No complete annual sequence of ordinary-model UK price lists was recovered here. “Core engines and gearboxes carried over” is justified; “nothing changed in 2010/2011/2012” is not established.
- Do not infer UK standard equipment from a US specification sheet or Dutch package names. The Black Edition content above is independently verified in the original UK price list.
- Original brochure publication date is not model year: preceding-generation brochure **05/07, ©2007, WVK 310 320 08 E/WW**; launch brochure **08/08, ©2008, WVK 314 725 09 GB/UK**; ordinary and Spyder brochures **01/10, ©2010**, respectively **WSLB1101000125** and **WSLB1101000225 GB/UK**; Black Edition brochure **11/10, ©2011, WSLS1201000525 GB/UK**. [Old brochure][old-brochure]; [Launch brochure][standard-2009]; [2010 ordinary brochure][standard-2010]; [Spyder brochure][spyder-brochure]; [Black brochure][black-brochure]
- Availability qualifiers in the launch brochure matter: sports exhaust **04/2009 at earliest** and mechanical differential **05/2009 at earliest, manual only**. These are planned dates in a prelaunch publication, not proof of actual first delivery. [UK launch brochure, p.107][standard-2009]
- Several old porsche.com press URLs now redirect, and files.porsche.com Club News URLs can return 404 despite indexed text. The collector-hosted original UK scans provide independently inspectable primary evidence; retain their colophons when archiving.
